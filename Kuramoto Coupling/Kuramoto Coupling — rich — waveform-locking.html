Path B, heard — the waveform is the coupling

A driver at 110 Hz and a follower tuned a little off an exact ratio above it. The follower is nudged by the driver's output — its waveform — not by its phase. The entry's claim: the harmonics the waveform carries decide which ratios can lock. A sawtooth has every harmonic, so it can pull the follower onto a true octave. A square wave has only odd ones, so at the octave there is nothing to grab — but at the twelfth there is. Hear whether the slow beating stops.
