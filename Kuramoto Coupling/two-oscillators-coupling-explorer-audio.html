Two oscillators, audio coupling explorer

Hear what the math says. Two audio-rate oscillators with slightly different natural frequencies. K is the coupling strength: at K = 0 they slide past each other and you hear the beating envelope as they drift in and out of phase. Raise K past K_c ≈ |fB − fA| / 2 and the beating slows, then stops: they lock to a shared frequency. Tap Start to unlock audio.

What the slider does: each animation frame (≈60 Hz) the page updates both oscillators' instantaneous frequency to fj + (K / 2π) · sin(θother − θj), Kuramoto's two-oscillator equation in the kinematic approximation. The audio thread reads the new frequencies through setValueAtTime(value, Tone.now()), which sidesteps Tone's musical-clock vs. UI-clock reconciliation — Tone treats parameter writes as wall-clock events, not bar-relative ones.
