Twenty-four voices, one knob

The instrument this section describes, in miniature: a population of oscillators whose coupling you perform. In pitch they are sine voices near 220 Hz — below the threshold a beating chorus, above it one tone, and it gets louder as it locks, because coherent voices add: the loudness you hear is R. In pulse they are fireflies ticking near once a second — scattered ticks that fall into a single clap.
