# Found ↔ Made

Two answers to a single question — *where does a thing's essence come from?*

**Found.** The essence is already there; the maker's job is to uncover it and get out of the way. [[Rick Rubin]]: the work is already finished, you clear away what hides it. [[George Nakashima]]: the tree already has the shape it wants to become — the split and the burl are messages, not flaws. The move is to *listen and subtract*, never impose.

**Made.** There is no waiting essence — only what you sing or build into being. A [[Songlines|songline]] *is* the path; stop singing it and the country goes dark, not geographically but ontologically. [[Donella Meadows]]' systems have no fixed self to return to; they are always becoming, and you don't uncover their nature, you intervene to bend their trajectory. The move is to *enact*, because nothing was there to find.

## The floor they share — material fidelity IS leverage

Set the poles against each other and they look like contraries — and the palace wires them as such: [[Rick Rubin]] `contradicts` [[Donella Meadows]] (discovered essence vs. systems becoming), [[George Nakashima]] `contradicts` [[Compressor Design]] (reveal the break vs. hide it). But stand under both and the same floor appears. The craftsman who yields to the grain and the systems-thinker who finds the one leverage point are doing the *identical* thing: **working with a system's given structure instead of overriding it.** Nakashima doesn't fight the split — he spans it with a butterfly key. Meadows doesn't force the system — she pushes where its own feedback rewards a small move.

So the two registers collapse into one claim: **true leverage lives exactly where a system's given structure resists brute force and rewards alignment.** Craft calls it fidelity to the material; systems thinking calls it the leverage point. Found and Made are the same discipline heard in two rooms — the woodshop and the world-model. What you cannot do, in either, is override the given and expect the result to hold.

This is the axis those contradictions live on: not a disagreement to resolve, but the single spectrum whose two ends the palace's makers keep standing at.
