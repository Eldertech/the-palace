# Loudon Live — Launch Kit

Copy and assets to get Stream #1 on the air. Focus: the foundations of sound, real music technique, and the craft of building instruments and effects. Tools stay loose. Tone: curious, technical, professional. WIP framing is a light touch in the trailer only.

---

## 1. Channel identity

**Channel name**

Loudon Live

**Tagline — pick one (or use as A/B options)**

1. Building synths, effects, and tools — out loud.
2. Sound from the ground up. Music alongside.
3. Sound design, music technique, and the occasional rabbit hole.
4. Where instruments get built, sound gets studied, and music gets played.

Recommended: #1. It signals the medium (live) and the work (building), and stays open enough to cover both the tech and the music itself.

**Banner text (one short line, plus schedule)**

> Loudon Live · synths, effects, and DSP, built on stream
> New streams [DAY] · [TIME] [TZ]

If you don't have a fixed schedule yet, drop the second line. Don't put a schedule up you can't keep.

## 3. Channel trailer script (~60 sec)

This is the one place the "figuring it out in real time" framing lives, per your direction. Light touch — one sentence, then move on.

**Trailer script**

> [On camera, plain background or workspace shot]
>
> Hi — I'm Loudon. This is Loudon Live.
>
> I build creative-technology projects — synths, effects, audio tools — across whatever tools fit the day. And I do most of that work live, on this channel, while talking through what I'm doing — not just the technical side, but the music itself. I've spent a long time teaching music, and that thinking comes along.
>
> The format is pretty simple. Each stream, I pick one project and push it forward. Some streams will be deep dives into the foundations of sound. Some will be hands-on builds. Some will be about technique — how a sound gets shaped, why a phrase works, what your ears are actually doing. Some will be me hitting a wall and figuring out how to get past it.
>
> A note up front: the channel itself is a work in progress. The structure, the schedule, the production — I'm dialing those in as I go. If something looks rough early on, that's why.
>
> If you like watching real work get made, hit subscribe. I'll see you on stream.

Length target: 45–75 seconds. Don't over-rehearse it. One take with a few mistakes left in is on-brand.

## 4. Stream titles & descriptions

You asked for vague, no-promise titles. These are pattern-based so you can name a stream in 30 seconds.

**Title patterns**

- `Live build · [project nickname]`
- `Sound lab · [topic]` (e.g., "Sound lab · what makes a sound feel warm")
- `Synth lab · session [N]`
- `Workshop stream · [project nickname]`
- `First principles · [topic]`
- `Open build · [thing] from scratch`

Avoid in titles, at least early on:
- Specific deliverables ("Finishing the…")
- Time promises ("…in one hour")
- Outcome promises ("Working synth by end of stream")

**Three example first titles (concrete, vague enough to keep you safe)**

1. Live build · synth lab, session 1
2. Sound lab · first principles
3. Open build · audio effect from scratch

**Stream description template**

> Live creative-tech build. Today's project: [one line, no promises].
>
> Tools today: [keep it loose — whatever fits the project].
>
> No outcome promises — this is a working stream. Hang out, ask questions in chat.
>
> ---
> About the channel: [paste your short About line]
> More on what I'm building: [optional, link if you have one]
>
> Chapters will be added after the stream ends.

The "Chapters added after" line buys you time and sets the right expectation for VOD viewers.

## 7. Media & asset list — MVP for Stream #1

This tier is everything you need to flip the switch on Stream #1 without anything looking broken or unfinished. Specs follow YouTube's current guidelines as of your launch window — double-check before exporting in case YouTube has shifted them.

**Channel-level (one-time setup)**

1. **Channel banner** — 2560×1440 px, PNG or JPG, ≤6 MB. Safe area for text/logo: 1546×423 px centered. Plain background, channel name, tagline, optional schedule line.
2. **Channel avatar / profile picture** — 800×800 px, PNG. Will be displayed as a circle, so keep important content centered and inside a 720 px circle.
3. **Channel watermark** — 150×150 px, transparent PNG. Sits in the corner of every video. Make this a tiny "LL" mark or a clean wordmark.

**OBS full-screen scene cards** (1920×1080 px PNG, transparent or solid, your choice)

4. `Starting Soon` card
5. `BRB` card
6. `Stream Ended / Thanks` card

You can make all three from one template — same layout, different headline. Keep type large enough to read on a phone. Leave a quiet zone at the bottom for chat overlay if you embed one.

**On-camera framing**

7. **Webcam frame / cam border** — transparent PNG sized to your cam placement (often 480×360 or 640×360 px). Optional but instantly looks more "produced." A simple 4–8 px border is enough.
8. **Lower-third name tag** — transparent PNG, ~600×100 px. `Loudon · Loudon Live`.

**Audio**

9. **One royalty-free background music loop** for the Starting Soon / BRB cards. 30–60 seconds, loopable, low-key. Sources: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, FreeMusicArchive, or your own. Do NOT use copyrighted music — even quietly. YouTube will catch it.
10. **Mic check sweep / line check tone** — optional, but useful. A 5-second 1 kHz tone or pink-noise burst you can play before going live to confirm levels.

**One-time content**

11. **Channel trailer video** — 45–75 sec. Use the script in §3. This sits at the top of your channel for non-subscribers. You can record this on Stream #1's setup day.

That's the MVP list. With those eleven items, you can go live tonight without anything looking embarrassing.

## 8. Media & asset list — Nice-to-have (first month)

Add these as you have time. None of them are blockers.

**Visual polish**

- Animated `Starting Soon` countdown (5-min countdown is the standard).
- Stream stinger / transition (1–2 sec) between scenes.
- Short channel intro animation (3–5 sec) at the start of VODs.
- A second scene background (e.g., a "talking head" scene vs. a "build" scene that shows your editor full-screen).
- Topic title card per stream — same template, swap the topic line. Builds visual consistency across VODs.
- Outro card with subscribe prompt + "next stream: [date]" line.
- Thumbnail template for VODs — 1280×720 px. Three or four variants you can swap a topic word into.

**Functional overlays**

- Chat overlay on stream (so chat is visible to VOD viewers later).
- Q&A / "chat question" callout overlay.
- "Code on screen" / "patch on screen" lower-third you can toggle when you switch to a screen-share scene.
- Stream alerts (new sub, new follow) if you want them — keep them quiet and short.

**Audio**

- A small library of 3–5 background loops you rotate so standby music doesn't get repetitive.
- A few short sound effects (scene change, alert, "let's go") — use sparingly.

**Channel maintenance**

- Pinned community post: a short "what this channel is" intro for first-time visitors.
- VOD chapter style guide for yourself, so chapters look consistent across streams.
- Playlist structure (e.g., "Sound from first principles," "Synth lab," "Music technique," "Live builds") — start adding VODs to playlists from stream #1 forward.
- A simple custom end screen layout you reuse on every VOD.

**Brand-y stuff (only if you want)**

- A wordmark / logo refinement past the MVP avatar.
- Custom emoji set (only matters once you turn on memberships).
- A short site or landing page that links the channel + any other Loudon Live work.

## 9. Pre-flight checklist for Stream #1

The minimum sequence to actually go live, in order:

1. Channel banner, avatar, watermark uploaded.
2. About / description filled in.
3. Three OBS scenes built: `Starting Soon`, `Live`, `BRB`. (Stream-end card optional but easy.)
4. Audio levels checked — mic, system audio, music loop.
5. One royalty-free music loop assigned to `Starting Soon` and `BRB`.
6. Stream key plugged into OBS, test stream done as Unlisted at least once.
7. Stream title and description drafted using the §4 template.
8. Going-live post drafted in advance (don't write it while live).
9. Phone or second device open to YouTube to confirm what viewers actually see.
10. Go.

Channel trailer can be recorded and uploaded after Stream #1 — it's higher-leverage once there's a VOD or two for new viewers to follow up with.
