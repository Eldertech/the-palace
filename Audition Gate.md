# Audition Gate

## 1. The Practice
*Section seed.* Before a sound-medium deliverable is accepted, the Maker (or the Specialist itself) plays it back and listens. The audition is non-negotiable. It is not a review of the spec; it is a check that the spec's intent landed in the audio. Multiple recent palace projects — [[Phoneme Choir]], [[Talking Keyboard]] — surfaced critical design errors at the audition gate that no spec review would have caught.

## 2. Why Listening Catches What Code Doesn't
*Section seed.* The compiler verifies the syntax; the unit test verifies the logic; the audition verifies the *result*. In a sound-medium project, the spec describes what should be true, but only the audio is the truth. Worth naming because the gap between spec and audio is where most musical failures live, and the audition is the only practice that exposes it.

## 3. Examples in the Palace
*Section seed.* Phoneme Choir: Kokoro syllabified words differently than the spec assumed; only audition caught the mismatch. Talking Keyboard: pronunciation of "eight" required spelling as "eigh" — audition was how this was discovered. Multiple Compressor Design lessons hinged on listening to the 500-sample window as time the student could feel. Each is an existence proof.

## 4. Sibling Gates
*Section seed.* If audition is the sound-medium gate, what are the gates for other media? The Look gate (image: does the rendered image actually carry the intent?). The Watch gate (motion: does the animation read as the spec described?). The Play gate (interactive: does the affordance show up in actual use?). Each is the same practice — *the test where the spec is suspended and perception takes over* — applied to a different sense.

## 5. Audition in the Stewardship Workflow
*Section seed.* [[Substrate Skill]]'s "stage as alignment confidence" frames a Stage 1 deliverable differently than Stage 5. The audition gate's threshold scales with stage: at Stage 1, audition asks "does it make sound?"; at Stage 5, audition asks "is it the sound Loudon's audience will hear?". The gate is the same; what counts as passing changes.

## Forward Vectors / Open Questions
- Should the audition gate become a required step in Specialist Job Contracts, or stay as a Maker-level discretion?
- What is the right artifact: an audition log (date, who listened, what they heard), or just the gotchas?
- Are there cases where audition lies — where what sounds good in isolation fails in the mix — and what is the antidote?
