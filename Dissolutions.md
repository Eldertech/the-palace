# Dissolutions

![[Dissolutions — hero.png]]

A pattern the palace keeps re-discovering: **two things taught as separate turn out to be one object, seen from different doorways.** Not analogies. Not isomorphisms in passing. *The same object*, with the apparent difference traceable entirely to how it is read — by phasor or by impulse, by round-trip or by scan, by slow envelope or by audio-rate waveform, by analysis or by synthesis. When the representation changes, the boundary disappears.

This hub holds the catalogue. Each member entry names one specific dissolution. The hub itself names the recurring move.

## The recurring move

A dissolution has three ingredients:

1. **Two frameworks** in active use, taught and reasoned-about as separate.
2. **A reframing** — typically a coordinate change, a basis change, a timescale rescaling, or a representational shift — that surfaces a single underlying object.
3. **A new design surface** that the dissolution unlocks: parameters that didn't exist as parameters before become controllable; questions answerable in one framework become answerable in the other.

The third ingredient is what distinguishes a dissolution from a clever observation. A dissolution is *productive*: it gives the practitioner new knobs.

## The catalogue (so far)

- **[[Dispersion Table]]** — the wavetable oscillator *is* the lossless waveguide loop. The two paradigms collapse into one object whose unclaimed axis is phase response, not amplitude.
- **[[Phase Reduction]]** — [[Floquet Theory]] *is* [[Kuramoto Coupling]] read forward; Kuramoto is the phase-reduced shadow of any population of limit cycles. The PRC is Floquet data.
- **[[Chebyshev is Fourier]]** — Chebyshev polynomials *are* Fourier basis under a coordinate change ($x = \cos\theta$). The orthogonality and the lattice are the same lattice in disguise.
- **[[Volterra Kernels and the Torus]]** — a synthesizer and an effect *are* the same kernel; what distinguishes them is whether phasors or input signals drive the read.
- **[[Exponential Decay Curvature]]** — an envelope *is* a waveform at audio rate, read at slow timescales. One equation, two operational frames.
- **[[Linear Predictive Coding]]** — analysis *is* synthesis read backward; the autoregressive filter that compresses a signal is the same filter that resynthesizes it.
- **[[Rank-N Lattice Analysis]]** — the minimal generating spectrum of a sound *is* the dual read of whatever field synthesized it. Analysis recovers the lattice synthesis used.

## What lives next door

This hub emerged from [[Boundary-Crossing Instruments]] (the breakthrough that named the pattern in its first instance — compression ratio as slope, the discontinuity living in the representation, not the phenomenon) and mirrors [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] (where separate pillars are revealed unified by a higher-dimensional coordinate system). The relationship to [[Frequency-Time Duality]] is the foundational example: one dial — repeat rate — produces pitch, echo, slapback, rhythm, and pulse, with no boundaries except the perceptual ones we draw.

## When the catalogue grows

A new entry earns membership in Dissolutions when it satisfies all three ingredients above. The Weave is the natural occasion to ask: *did anything new dissolve this cycle?* If two recent entries have started reading like the same object, propose the third — the entry that names the dissolution — and link them in.
