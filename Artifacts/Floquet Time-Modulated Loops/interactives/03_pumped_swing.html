The Pumped Swing

This is the parametrically-pumped pendulum: θ̈ + 2ζ ω₀ θ̇ + (ω₀² + ap Ω² cos(Ωt)) sin θ = 0. Same structure as the Mathieu equation. The "swing" is the pendulum; the "pump" modulates the effective length, which shows up as a periodic coefficient on the restoring term.
