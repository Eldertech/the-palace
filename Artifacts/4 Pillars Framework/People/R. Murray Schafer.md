# R. Murray Schafer
## Musician/Composer

**Domain:** Musician/Composer

---

## Key Works

- The Soundscape: Our Sonic Environment and the Tuning of the World (1977)
- Ear Cleaning (educational)
- The Music of the Environment (essay)

---

## Core Concepts

- **Soundscape** - all sounds in environment as composition
- **Schizophonia** - split between sound and source
- **Hi-fi vs lo-fi soundscapes** - signal-to-noise ratio in environments
- **Keynote sounds** - background sounds that define a place
- **Ear cleaning** - listening exercises to heighten awareness

---

## Connection to 4 Pillars

Schafer teaches that the world is already music - we just need to listen. Recording/production is soundscape curation. Field recording ↔ Schafer's deep listening. Every space has a sonic signature. His 'ear cleaning' exercises are meditation for sound designers.

---

## Key Quotes

> "The soundscape is any acoustic field of study. We may speak of a musical composition as a soundscape, or a radio program as a soundscape, or an acoustic environment as a soundscape."
> "I want people to realize that sounds are all around us and that listening is just as important as sound making."
> "Noise is any sound we have learned to ignore."

---

## Recommended Reading Approach

Read The Soundscape while doing soundwalks - go outside, record, listen. His concepts click when you apply them to real environments. Do his ear cleaning exercises daily.

## Applications to Four Pillars

### Creation Pillar
Field recording as composition. Soundscape curation. Environmental listening informs production aesthetic.

### Tools Pillar
Record room tones, impulse responses. Build soundscape library. Max patch for soundscape analysis.

### Philosophy Pillar
Everything is already music. Production = selection + framing, not creation ex nihilo.

### Practice Pillar
Daily soundwalk: 10 minutes listening without recording. Notice keynote, signals, soundmarks.
