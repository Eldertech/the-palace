# Sam Maloof
## Maker/Craftsman

**Domain:** Maker/Craftsman

---

## Key Works

- Rocking chairs (signature work)
- Sam Maloof: Woodworker (book)
- Hand-shaped furniture

---

## Core Concepts

- **Hand-shaped forms** - no straight lines, all curves
- **Comfort through craft** - form follows function perfectly
- **Repetition with variation** - made 1000+ chairs, each unique
- **Workshop as life** - making as practice, not career
- **Teaching through demonstration** - show, don't tell

---

## Connection to 4 Pillars

Maloof made the same rocking chair design for 50 years - each one different. Sound familiar? (Martin's grids, four-on-the-floor, etc.) His hand-shaping = parameter automation in DAW - subtle curves, no hard edges. Comfort through craft = ergonomic interfaces, musical workflow design.

---

## Key Quotes

> "I never made two chairs exactly alike. Each one is a little different from the one before."
> "I have worked wood all my life... I have never been bored."
> "The wood teaches you how to make it."

## Applications to Four Pillars

### Creation Pillar

### Tools Pillar
Hand-craft your tools. Customize every interface. Make it ergonomic for YOUR workflow.

### Philosophy Pillar
Making as life practice. Never bored with repetition because attention stays fresh.

### Practice Pillar
Pick one technique, explore it for a year. 365 variations. Notice endless depth.
