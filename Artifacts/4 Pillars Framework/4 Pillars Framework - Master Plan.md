## Vision

The 4 Pillars Framework is a comprehensive educational approach integrating:
1. **Music Production/Synthesis** (Creation pillar)
2. **Composition/Musicianship** (Creation pillar)
3. **Technology/Max/Systems** (Tools pillar)
4. **Philosophy/Creative Wellbeing** (Philosophy & Practice pillars)

**Core Principle:** These aren't separate subjects—they're the same patterns in different materials. Teaching cross-domain pattern recognition creates transferable thinking.

**Paradigm Shift:** Quality = Wellbeing/Integrity/Depth, not metrics/reach/money (highest-leverage intervention per Donella Meadows)
