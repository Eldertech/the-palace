## The Technical Side

### What Is Reverb?

**Acoustic reality:** When sound reflects off surfaces, creating:
- Early reflections (first 50ms)
- Reverb tail (diffuse reflections)
- Decay time (RT60 - time to decay 60dB)

**In nature:**
- Small room: Short reverb, bright
- Large hall: Long reverb, complex
- Cathedral: Very long reverb, sacred feeling

### Reverb Types

**Algorithmic:**
- Mathematical simulation
- Adjustable parameters
- Artificial but controllable

**Convolution:**
- Based on impulse response from real space
- Authentic sound
- Less flexible

**Spring/Plate:**
- Mechanical reverb
- Vintage character
- Musical imperfection

### The Parameters

**Pre-delay:** Gap between direct sound and reverb
- 0ms: Close mic feeling
- 20-50ms: Natural distance
- 100ms+: Slapback echo

**Decay time:** How long reverb lasts
- 0.5s: Small room
- 2s: Medium hall
- 5s+: Cathedral

**Early reflections:** First reflections reveal room size/shape

## The Conceptual Side

### Heidegger: Building Dwelling Thinking

**Key insight:** Building creates space for dwelling (living).

**Not:**
- Space as empty container
- Architecture as decoration

**But:**
- Space as place for being
- Building as creating dwelling

**In music:** Reverb creates sonic dwelling space.

### The Fourfold (Geviert)

Heidegger: Dwelling unites earth, sky, mortals, divinities.

**In reverb:**
- **Earth:** Direct sound (grounded, present)
- **Sky:** Reverb tail (ethereal, infinite)
- **Mortals:** Performer (human scale)
- **Divinities:** Transcendent feeling (cathedral)

**Sacred spaces:** Architecture designed for transcendence through space.

### Lucier's Revelation

"I Am Sitting in a Room" (1969):
- Record voice reading text in room
- Play recording back into same room, record again
- Repeat until only room resonances remain
- **The room's voice emerges**

**What this proves:** Space has signature. Reverb reveals it.

## Teaching the Resonance

### Part 1: Record Impulse Response
**Assignment:** Capture a real space

**Method:**
1. Choose interesting room (bathroom, stairwell, parking garage)
2. Generate impulse (clap, balloon pop, starter pistol)
3. Record in high quality
4. Use as convolution IR
5. Apply to dry signal

**The revelation:** Your reverb now IS that space.

### Part 2: Three Spaces
**Assignment:** Same composition, three different spaces

**Process:**
1. Create dry mix (no reverb)
2. Version A: Cathedral IR (long, diffuse)
3. Version B: Bedroom IR (short, intimate)
4. Version C: No reverb (direct)

**Compare:** How does space change emotional impact?

### Part 3: Pre-delay and Distance
**Assignment:** Use pre-delay to control perceived distance

**Experiment:**
- 0ms pre-delay: Singer in your ear
- 30ms: Singer across room
- 100ms: Singer in large space

**The principle:** Time delay = perceived distance.

## Common Student Discoveries

### "Too much reverb makes everything muddy"

**Diagnosis:** Reverb masking direct signal.

**Solutions:**
- High-pass reverb (remove mud frequencies)
- Shorter decay times
- Less wet signal
- **Pre-delay creates separation**

### "Burial's reverb sounds 'wrong' but perfect"

**Observation:** Untrue uses unnatural reverb settings.

**Why it works:**
- Reverb as emotional effect, not realism
- Creates haunted, liminal atmosphere
- **Rules broken intentionally**

**Lesson:** Reverb doesn't have to be realistic to be musical.

### "I recorded my bathroom and it sounds amazing"

**Discovery:** Bathrooms have natural reverb.

**Why:**
- Hard surfaces (tile, porcelain)
- Small space (dense reflections)
- Natural high-frequency boost

**Application:** Tile bathrooms = free reverb chamber.
