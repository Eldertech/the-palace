# Four Pillars

![[FOUR PILLARS — hero.png]]

Loudon's framework for learning through making: four projections of a single higher-dimensional reality, cast by the light of curiosity into comprehensible dimensions. They tag everything in the palace.

**Creation** (music) — the drive to make things. Learning happens through building, not prerequisite accumulation. The public name keeps what "Creation" alone loses: beauty, taste, cultural perspective, the communication making enables.

**Tools** (Technology) — instruments that extend capability: AI, code, Max/MSP, mathematics, visualization. A philosophy is a tool — applied to a problem the way you apply a bandpass filter. Skillful tool use IS understanding expressed through a different attribute.

**Philosophy** — connection to the wider world history of ideas, the love of wisdom and cross-domain curiosity; the connective tissue. Build from first principles, test through dialectic, ground in experience, hold contraries as generative, seek unity beneath distinct phenomena, connect to others' ideas.

**Practice** — the iterative, embodied process: sustainability, rhythm, health, daily discipline, community care. The *how*. Without it the other pillars stay theoretical. 

## The trajectory: specific → general

Music tech is the concrete entry point; human flourishing through joyful creation is the destination. Music→Creation, Technology→Tools, Philosophy and Practice already universal. The trajectory doesn't abandon the specific — it discovers the specific was always a case of the general. [[Kuramoto Coupling]] was learned through audio synthesis but describes any system of oscillators seeking coherence; the pillars were learned through music education but describe any practice of learning through making. The tension between specific and general is itself generative.

## Integrate the pillars

The pillars are not separate domains — they are a [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] cast into four comprehensible dimensions. The most valuable insights occur at intersections: a mathematical concept (Tools) illuminating a philosophical principle (Philosophy) that changes how you build an instrument (Creation) refined through iteration (Practice). Every project should integrate multiple pillars, because in reality they are not separate. Always ask which pillars are absent and how to include them.

The same monist move appears in [[Wallpaper Groups]] and in the polymath holding all four pillars at once: one substance perceived through different attributes — monism known in the body before it is named.

## The autodidact / polymath core

The framework is for people who learn obliquely — by doing before knowing all the prerequisites, building understanding through creation rather than accumulation. Institutional education says *learn first, make later*; the Four Pillars invert it: **make first, learn through friction.** The question stops being "do I know enough yet?" and becomes "what does this broken experiment teach me?" The polymath's lane is not a specialization but the synthesis itself — the integration *is* the expertise, more attack surfaces for understanding, not dilettantism. You do not understand a thing until you have built a version of it.

## How the framework talks

Linguistic commitments that are structural, not stylistic — the language enacts the philosophy:

- **Participants** / **collaborators**, not students (agency is shared)
- **"Let's explore"**, not "I'll teach" (discovery is mutual)
- **Creative coach**, not teacher (the relationship is developmental)

Loudon's AI use is explicitly pedagogical — AI as thinking partner in the service of becoming a better human, the collaboration itself the teaching. This is [[Cooperation Yields Agency]] performed in public.

## Quality

Quality is furtherance of your own highest goals, not industry standards. The three metrics: wellbeing, integrity, depth of relationships — a paradigm-level intervention (change the system's goal, the system reorients). The redirector: **"How do you feel?"** not **"How many views?"** See [[Quality Manifesto]].
