# Sandbox — Coupling Constant

The coupling constant *K* is the strength of influence each oscillator exerts on the others.
It is the control knob of synchronization: turn it up and the population entrains; turn it down
and they scatter into independent drift.

The Kuramoto model that formalized this was introduced by Yoshiki Kuramoto in 1985, and the
critical coupling *K_c* has been the object of study ever since. Below *K_c*, incoherence;
above it, a growing synchronized cluster.
