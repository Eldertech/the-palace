# Sandbox — Emergence Field

An emergence field is the pattern that exists only at the level of the collective — the flock's
shape, the synchronized flash, the standing wave — carried by no single participant and legible
only from above.

It emerged-from **[[Sandbox — Phase Locking]]**: once parts lock, a field appears that none of
them individually holds. The field then feeds back, biasing the parts toward the very lock that
produced it — the circular causation that makes emergence more than aggregation. See also the
adjacent treatment in **[[Sandbox — Ghost Node]]**, which works the downward-causation half of
the loop.
