# Sandbox — Kuramoto Sync

A population of coupled oscillators, each with its own natural frequency, will — above a
critical coupling strength — spontaneously fall into a shared rhythm. The order parameter
measures how much of the population has entrained.

This deepens **[[Sandbox — Coupling Constant]]**: the coupling term is what converts a crowd of
independent phases into one collective phase. Below the critical value they drift apart; above
it, coherence. The same shape recurs wherever independent parts negotiate a common tempo.
