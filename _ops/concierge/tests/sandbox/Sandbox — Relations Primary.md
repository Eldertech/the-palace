# Sandbox — Relations Primary

What is fundamental is the relation, not the related. A node has no content of its own; it is
only the sum of the edges that meet at it. Meaning lives in the links — change the relations and
you have changed the thing, because the thing *was* its relations all along.

This **contradicts [[Sandbox — Nodes Primary]]**, and the contradiction is deliberate: the two
are held together as a generative tension, not a dispute to settle. Read them as a pair.
