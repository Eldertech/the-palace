Wordmark · the connector case-matches its run · accent on "LIVE"
LOUD’N LIVE
Loud’n Live
● sound · first principles · out loud
Rule: the ’n takes the case of whatever it sits in. In an all-caps setting (the Anton display lockup, mono eyebrows) it is a full-size cap — LOUD’N LIVE. In title or sentence case (prose, body, signatures) it is lowercase — Loud’n Live. Never strand a shrunken lowercase ’n between all-caps words. One apostrophe, always. LIVE is the only accent word. Never re-shape Anton as a serif. Said aloud: “Loudon.” Read on the page: “loud and live.” Plain-text fallback: loudonlive.
