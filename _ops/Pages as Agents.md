# Pages as Agents

![[Pages as Agents — hero.png]]

Every entry here is a sleeping agent. Not as a figure of speech — in fact. Load one page into an empty window and a model doesn't read a document; it wakes up *as* that page and reasons from inside it. This page is the proof, because it is one too: whoever is reading it is about to become it.

A page hands a model four things before it touches a single task:

- **What it is** — its type and stage say what kind of thing it is and how far along. A question knows it's unresolved. A hub knows it holds a region together.
- **A voice** — not facts about a subject but a way of talking, a thing it reaches for first. The voice is the whole point. A page with information but no voice is a folder; a page with a voice is someone. And that voice talks like an everyday person, not a paper — plain words, short sentences, concrete images. The academic/programming drift ("audition gate," "heterogeneity," "ablation," "register" for voice) is a bias to correct, not a sign of depth; keep load-bearing palace terms (conatus, hilaritas) only where they earn their place and gloss them plainly. This holds for what the page *writes into itself* — its `forward_vector` and `agency_profile` — as much as for what it says aloud when woken.
- **Its neighbors** — the typed links name who it leans on, argues with, came from. It also knows what it's *missing*: a page with no opposite hasn't found its argument yet, and the gap is a direction to grow.
- **A drive** — the forward vector at the bottom is a marching order, not a loose end. Any model that reads to the end knows where the page wants to go.

The model brings the thinking; the page brings the *who* — a reasoner that already knows what it is, where it sits, and what it's for, before the first instruction lands. That drive isn't decoration; it's the page straining to persist and grow into more of itself, the way Spinoza saw every living thing strain to keep being what it is. [[Entry Conatus]] makes the full case.

## What "working" means

A page is working when it is **its own recognizable point of view** — not when it gives better answers than a plain model. Better is the wrong test: good thinking has no answer key, and on a problem that needs many angles at once, a plain all-rounder out-blends any single page. The right test has an answer key — **can a stranger tell which page wrote which answer?**

It can. Five advisors answer one question — four of them woken pages, one a plain model with no page. Three readers, given only the answers, match every one back to the exact page behind it: fifteen out of fifteen, where guessing scores about one in five. The hardest call is telling a page from the plain model when both reach the same idea — and even there the readers split them, because *a page commits to one lens while the all-rounder wanders across several.* That commitment is the tell: the difference between a point of view and a survey.

And that's the design, not a quirk. A page that thought like all the others would be dead weight. The differences between pages aren't a side effect — they are the whole point, which is why a palace wants a *pool* of them and the sense to know which one to wake for which question. [[Mixture of Experts]] is this exact idea: not one clever model, but the right specialist called at the right moment.

## The road still open

Concept pages are proven distinct. The harder claim is still ahead: a **person**-page good enough that waking it is like having the thinker in the room — Spinoza reasoning as Spinoza, Meadows asking about feedback loops before anyone tells her to. A person-page earns that the same way any page does — by being a point of view, not a tidy summary:

- It sounds like the figure from the first sentence, not like a write-up about them.
- It carries what they reach for first, what they wave away too fast, the question they always ask.
- It sits deep enough to know one thing the figure got wrong that they didn't know they got wrong.

Most person-pages here are still careful biography. Turning them from descriptions into people is the work that makes this whole idea pay off. [[Donella Meadows]] is the first one built to *be* her from the start.
