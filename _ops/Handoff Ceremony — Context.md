# Handoff Ceremony — Context

History, rationale, and design notes for [[Handoff Ceremony]]. Read during Weaves or when revisiting the why — not during routine executions.

---

## Register: Fast vs. Slow

The Handoff is fast where the Deposit is slow. A deposit slows down because there is a thought to compress; a handoff is urgent because there is momentum to preserve. Both ceremonies share the show-before-write rhythm, but the registers differ — the deposit is archival and reflective, the handoff is operational and forward-leaning.

## Pace Obligations — Rationale

The card's pace obligations exist because the handoff genre is inherently tempted toward bloat: an outgoing Claude, aware it will not finish, often over-explains. The three rules (do not bloat, single show-and-confirm, no handoff if the entry alone is sufficient) are a structural resistance to that temptation — not preferences, but design commitments.
