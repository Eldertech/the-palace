A feedback delay network whose tubes are slime-mold paths. Click a yellow food-flake to inject a pluck; the signal travels the tubes (each a delay line), echoes back through its own feedback loop, and drains into the red sink — your speakers. This is the audio engine alone, the smallest version that exercises every parameter, built in the browser before any RNBO port. You watch; you do not draw the routes. The mold owns the topology.
