Action Potential Oscillator · cycle 4 · browser model of neuropulse.dsp

This is the audible target for the Faust population engine. The same Stage-4 neuron — charge, spike-rise, spike-fall, refractory recovery — runs here as a crowd of N neurons, coupled by the identical mean-field rule the .dsp uses: each neuron's drive current gets a nudge of K × (mean of who is spiking right now). Below a critical K the crowd is noise; above it the crowd snaps into a phase-locked drone. Press Play, then sweep K and listen for the lock.

Each row = one neuron · each mark = a spike · diagonal/vertical alignment = the crowd agreeing. Bar at right = synchrony r (green when locked).

Honest limits of this model vs. the .dsp: it runs at a downsampled control rate for the visual raster and uses the same linear spike ramps and spring-mass recovery as the Faust source, so the timbre is a faithful sketch, not a sample-exact twin. The Faust engine runs every neuron at full audio rate with one-sample mean-field delay; here the mean-field is computed once per control block. The transition K-range and the lock behavior should match by ear — that is the thing to A/B once the Mac compile lands.
