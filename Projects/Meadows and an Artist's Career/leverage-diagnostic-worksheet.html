The diagnostic, in one line: most artists spend ~80% of their attention at level 12 and ~1% at levels 1–4 — the leverage is upside down. Your placements run the opposite way: the bottom of the ladder is where you've stopped looking, and your real work lives in levels 1–8. That is itself the finding worth testing — either you've genuinely climbed, or the Manifesto describes who you intend to be more than where today's hours actually go. The blank column is where you settle that. Fill in one row where the placement is wrong, and that single correction will teach the page more than the whole interview.
