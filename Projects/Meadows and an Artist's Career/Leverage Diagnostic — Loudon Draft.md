# Leverage Diagnostic — Loudon Draft

> **How to read this.** Each row is one of Meadows' twelve leverage points, ordered low-leverage → high-leverage. The **Steward's Draft** column is my best guess at where you are on that level, sourced from the palace (cited inline). The **Your Correction** column is yours to overwrite — strike out, rewrite, sharpen, contradict. The interesting signal is the gap between the two columns.

> Companion HTML printable: `leverage-diagnostic-worksheet.html` (in this bundle). This markdown version is the editable one.

## LOW LEVERAGE — the operational floor (80% of most artists' attention)

### 12 · Constants & Parameters
**Career question:** *What am I charging? What gear am I using? When am I posting?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice, from palace):**
I don't live here. Gear got chosen long ago and I stopped fiddling, pricing gets set once, thumbnails get whatever attention I can stand. 2 hours on a thumbnail is a no, daily metric-checking is a no. The trap is client work, where somebody else's standards drag me back down into parameter-land.

**Your correction:**
> I sometimes must get sucked into these when doing work for clients, they demand, or I hold myself to, a certain standard. Try not to take jobs full of this kind of work.

### 11 · Buffer Sizes
**Career question:** *How much savings? How many gigs / albums / lessons ahead?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice):**
Berklee Online is my financial buffer and it has a clock on it — call it five years of fade. The buffer isn't there to be hoarded, it's there to buy the time to build the next chapter. Peace is the ultimate luxury, financial comfort buys choice — use the runway. I'm also building social and attention buffers right now with Emily, family, and the nearest friends, because those compound differently than money.

**Your correction:**
> _Attention with family, social buffers and favors. I am building those up right now with my nearest friends and family. I have a financial buffer, that will dwindle over time, lets assume a 5 year fade out from here, and this buffer will help to extend that. I am not in a rush to swap income streams, but I can't rely on Berklee for my entire life, I need another financial chapter. **Take full advantage of the buffer** my previous career provides to launch the new one.

### 10 · Structure of Stocks & Flows
**Career question:** *What is the ratio of paid work to creative work? Of teaching to making?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice):**
The shape right now: Berklee Online is the stable inflow that funds the freedom for everything else. Loudon Live is the new outflow being grown into a weekly cadence around a community. Trading Berklee hours for Loudon Live hours over the next several years — not dissolving anything completely, just rebalancing everything everywhere.

**Your correction:**
> _Live a productive happy life and a part of the life makes money. Berklee Online is stable inflow that funds the freedom to redesign the rest or now.  It will naturally fade out over time, and I will need something new with similar strength to fade in. The Loudon Live will have a deliberate weekly cadence focused on supporting a community. At some point I trade the time spent at berkleeonline for time spent on Loudon Live.

### 9 · Delays
**Career question:** *How long from idea to ship? From decision to first action?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice):**
Long delays on finishing a course or grading a backlog are where the stress lives, that pile-up is the failure mode. Loudon Live is  a delay-killer by design: it records and it's done, more can be done, but something is done. 

**Your correction:**
> _ A PRIMARY reason to do a live stream is to remove the delay that plagues my creative work. It is recorded and done, more can be done, but something was done. The long delay in creating a course or having to grade that piles up, this is where my stress lies, remove that delay i propose will keep the stress away.

### 7 · Reinforcing Feedback Loops
**Career question:** *What pulls me forward when momentum builds? What makes the next step easier than the last?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice):**
Practice → Commitment → Integrity → Wellbeing → better practice. That's the named loop. The fast ones I feel day to day: a student lighting up pulls me forward, and an entry in the Palace makes the next entry easier because the connections compound. Loudon Live's small-room audience is the slower one — depth attracts depth, and once a community forms, enthusiasm is infectious and pulls me along. Technical systems can be fine control on these loops, a throttle.

**Your correction:**
> _Student attention pulls me forward, those interactions and excitements are reinforcing, once it gets going and a community forms, it will pull me along enthusiasm is infectious. Technical systems can function as fine control of these loops, pushing them forward or pulling back from the social. The Palace will reinforce, I am learning to make quality quickly, my creations here will mature at perfect times.

### 6 · Information Flows
**Career question:** *Who tells me what's actually happening? Which metric do I trust?*

**Steward's draft (rewritten in your voice):**
I trust no one fully and I test everything. I build up from mathematics to find what I'm willing to call true. Emily, the right hundred, the one student transformed — those are signals. View counts, follower counts, algorithm timing — not signals. The Palace is a slow mirror of where my thinking actually is, built in dialogue with AI, and I invite the world into it. YouTube conversations are how I judge health. The feel of the room at Loudon Live will be a signal I trust.

**Your correction:**
> _I trust no one fully, I am always skeptical of all information. I test my assumptions. I build up from mathematics to find my confident truth. My grounded statements. The palace started this way, and is becoming more. The palace tries to push my horizon. I invite the world into my palace. Where we have a long-cycle information flow — a slowly accumulating mirror of where my thinking actually is created with AI dialogue. Youtube conversations with people is how I judge health, I trust this as a real positive sign. Conversations during loudon live will be the trusted signal, the feeling I get in the room.
