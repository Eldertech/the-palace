# Biomechanical Synthesis

A synthesis aesthetic and philosophy that treats biological processes as DSP operations — not as metaphor but as design principles. The aesthetic is deliberately grotesque, visceral, alive. Body horror is pedagogy: making DSP mechanics physical makes them unforgettable.

## Eight Synthesizer Concepts

The framework is concrete. Eight instruments, eight biological systems:

1. **Neural oscillator** → audio waveform (direct membrane voltage mapping). Built and documented in [[Action Potential Oscillator]].

2. **Crystal resonator synthesis** — lattice vibrational modes as partial frequencies, crystal geometry as filter topology. The spatial arrangement of atoms becomes the spatial arrangement of harmonics.

3. **Mycelial network synthesis** — spawn points as initial frequencies, hyphae as spectral routing paths growing toward frequency-rich regions. Fruiting bodies emerge where hyphae concentrate. Chemical signals (envelope followers) trigger growth patterns. The network topology evolves according to simple rules; complexity emerges from local interactions.

4. **Blood hydraulics compressor** — blood pressure = amplitude, vessel diameter = gain reduction, pulse rate = attack time. The compressor physically demonstrates its own function.

5. **Slime mold delay** — the [[**plasmodium**]]'s foraging pattern as feedback delay topology, path optimization as reverb decay.

6. **Carnivorous plant gate** — Venus flytrap trigger threshold as noise gate with biological hysteresis.

7. **Bioluminescent phase bank** — chemiluminescent reaction timing mapped to phase relationships; the phase bank "glows" when frequencies are coherent.

8. **Cochlear implant vocoder** — direct mapping of cochlear mechanics to vocoder filter bank.

Each instrument is built not to mimic biology but to *become* biology. The DSP models the organism's biology. Use the Neural Synthesizer and Oscillator as examples of how to develop these concepts. ## The Mycelial Model in Detail

The mycelial architecture is the most developed:

**Spawn points** = initial frequencies (the notes played or the fundamental). These are not oscillators in the traditional sense; they are seeds in a biological field.

**Hyphae** = spectral paths that grow toward frequency-rich regions. Guided by spectral analysis of the input signal, the network extends threads into the frequency domain, following concentration gradients like a real mycelium follows nutrient gradients. Quickly toward nearest harmonic frequency of played MIDI note.

**Fruiting bodies** = resonant peaks that emerge where hyphae concentrate. Comb filters placed at intersection points. As the network grows, fruiting bodies appear, disappear, intensify, decay.

**Chemical signals** = envelope followers and cross-frequency detection. When one frequency region activates, it sends signals that affect growth in neighboring regions.

**Stability and chaos** = some patterns are stable attractors, some oscillate, some chaotic. A reverb tail does not just decay; it *grows and dies* in different regions of the spectrum. The reverb has morphology.

This is not a reverb that happens to sound organic. It is a reverb that *is* an organism.

## Visceral Pedagogy

The principle: "Visceral engagement — the tool creates discomfort that forces you to feel the signal processing. You'll never forget how a compressor works after pumping blood through arteries."

The body-horror aesthetic is not decoration. It is mandatory pedagogy. A student who builds a hydraulic blood compressor understands gain reduction not as a number but as a vessel narrowing under pressure. They feel it. The disgust is intentional — it burns the concept into memory.

When students encounter the carnivorous plant gate, they must design the threshold and hysteresis as biological parameters: how sensitive is the trigger? How long does the trap stay closed? These are DSP questions answered through biological intuition. The gate becomes unforgettable.

## DAW as Narrative World

Each track in the DAW becomes one character in a scene moving through time. The environment is a toroidal body-mod tattoo shop — bodies being transformed, inscribed, made strange. The DAW is not a workspace; it is a world.

Each track's EQ curve is this character's resonant frequencies — their "voice" in both senses. The compression is their breath, the attack time the speed of their breathing. The reverb is the space their voice occupies. The effects are their nervous system firing.

A mix is not a balance of levels but a choreography of organisms coexisting in the same space. When tracks interact — when one track's envelope modulates another's filter — they are touching. They are coupled, in the sense of [[Kuramoto Coupling]].

The narrative emerges from the physics. The student does not compose first and then find the story; the topology of the signal flow suggests the story.

## Cross-Domain Resonance

Biomechanical Synthesis [[mirrors]] [[Boundary-Crossing Instruments]] — the recognition that signal flow, biological flow, and narrative flow are the same pattern in different materials. A mycelium and a reverb tail and a character arc follow the same topology: local interactions creating emergent global form.

This connects to [[Hilaritas Generator]] through the discipline of delight: the horror is not for its own sake but for the joy of unexpected understanding. When a student suddenly grasps that a compressor is a blood pressure differential, that moment of visceral recognition is a spike of *hilaritas* — delight.

## The Neural Network Synthesis Paradigm

The most operational of the biological synthesis concepts is the neural network as synthesis architecture. Neural dynamics map directly to audio signal flow:

**Excitatory connections** — one neuron firing makes others more likely to fire → cascading avalanches of sound. A single note trigger can cascade through an excitatory network to produce complex polyrhythmic textures without explicit sequencing. The synthesis emerges from propagation.

**Inhibitory connections** — one neuron firing SUPPRESSES others → competitive rhythmic patterns, syncopation. Inhibitory networks create rhythms through absence — a beat emerges from what is *prevented* from firing. This is **rhythm as negative space**. In synthesis terms: a single trigger, when it reaches an inhibitory node, silences competing frequency regions, creating syncopated polyrhythm from sculptural silence.

**Feedback loops** — neurons feeding back into themselves or each other → self-sustaining oscillation, instability, drone. The **feedback loop IS the oscillator**; the oscillation frequency is the loop's natural resonance. Coupled feedback creates frequency locking and competition—[[Kuramoto Coupling]]-like behavior emerging from local interactions.

**At high rates (100+ Hz)**: dense granular textures approach continuous tones but retain temporal **microstructure** — like FM synthesis creating sidebands through rapid modulation. The neural firing rate becomes the grain rate. The instantaneous firing pattern becomes the grain shape.

The DAW-as-narrative-world emerges from this: "Each track in my DAW is one character in a scene that moves through time. Many concepts coexist together, in an alien landscape, Larry Niven meets M.C. Escher—with neural topology as the organizational principle. Tracks are neurons. Their connections (sends, sidechain modulations, frequency interactions) are synapses. The mix is a network firing."
