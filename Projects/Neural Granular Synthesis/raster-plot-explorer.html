The Raster Plot

Neural Granular Synthesis · population coherence, made visible

Each row is one neuron. Each dot is a spike at the instant it fired. You are not looking at a waveform — you are looking at a crowd. Turn the coupling up and the scatter snaps into vertical stripes: the population has agreed. The number that measures that agreement is r, the synchrony index, printed live below.

Vertical stripes — synchrony, the crowd is phase-locked (r → 1).   Random scatter — incoherence, no coupling (r → 0).   Diagonal bands — a traveling wave, the mean phase ψ marching across the population. The teal trace along the bottom is r itself over the last few seconds.
