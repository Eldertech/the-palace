The Mirror

The LPC lattice filter and the Kelly-Lochbaum tube are the same machine read from opposite ends.

A ladder of reflection coefficients ki is, at once, an LPC lattice filter and a cascade of acoustic-tube scattering junctions. Levinson-Durbin walks that ladder out of a recorded signal — analysis, from the output backward. The area relation walks the same ladder out of a tube's geometry — synthesis, from the shape forward. Two procedures, one object. The vocal tract and the plucked string are one piece of mathematics wearing two bodies. Below, a real Kelly-Lochbaum waveguide voices the six fitted vowels; the tube and the lattice sit side by side, and the number printed at each joint is the same number in both views. That shared number is the mirror.

Read it both ways

the same ladder, walked from two directions

The ladder of reflection coefficients ki does not care which way you arrived at it. One path starts with a recorded sound and recovers the tube that must have made it. The other starts with a tube and builds the filter that voices it. Both produce the identical ladder — that is the whole claim.

← Analysis · from the output

Levinson-Durbin walks k out of a signal

recorded signal→autocorrelation→recursion→ki
You have the output and want the tube. Take the speech, compute its autocorrelation r, and run the Levinson-Durbin recursion. It peels the reflection ladder off the signal one rung at a time — each ki is the partial correlation the lower-order model could not already explain.
reflection from prediction error
ki = ri+1 − Σ aj ri−jEi
reflection = (next correlation − what the model predicts) ÷ remaining prediction error
Then the step-up recursion turns that ladder into the all-pole denominator A(z), whose poles are the formants — the resonances you heard. Analysis ran the machine backward: sound in, geometry out.
Synthesis · from the geometry →

Geometry walks k out of tube areas

tube areas Ai→adjacent ratio→ki
You have the tube and want the sound. The vocal tract is a chain of cylindrical sections with areas Ai. At every joint, the impedance mismatch between the section before and the section after sets the reflection directly — no recursion, just the neighbours.
reflection from area mismatch
ki = Ai − Ai+1Ai + Ai+1
reflection = (area before − area after) ÷ (area before + area after)
Feed that ladder a glottal pulse through the Kelly-Lochbaum junctions and out comes the vowel. Synthesis ran the same machine forward: geometry in, sound out. Same ki.

The lattice you hover on the right and the tube you hover on the left print the same number at the same joint because they are not two models of one thing — they are one operator with two front doors. Analysis enters from the sound; synthesis enters from the shape; the ladder in the middle is shared. That shared ladder is the mirror.
