Portamento by Ear

single-session reel · ~15 minutes · 6 clips

Six glides. For each: listen twice, name the regime in your head (overdamped · smooth arrival, no overshoot  |  critically damped · fastest no-overshoot landing  |  underdamped · overshoots and rings back), then guess the physical system. Reveal. Move on. The first round is unambiguous; the second sharpens the ear.

Round one — clear signatures
01
A slow brass glide. Which regime?
Reveal
Overdamped
Trombone — heavy slide, long air column, brass damps overshoot.
Smooth exponential approach. No "thump" of arrival, no ring. The slide's mass and the air column's loss together push ζ well above 1.
02
A synth glide that lands fast and clean.
Reveal
Critically damped
Synthesizer — engineered glide circuit tuned for the fastest no-overshoot arrival.
The shortest path that does not ring. ζ=1 is an engineered target; circuits like this are built to it.
03
A bell struck slightly off, settling toward its true pitch.
Reveal
Underdamped
Bell — light strike that detunes the mode; resonance rings around the target.
The pitch swings past the target and oscillates around it while decaying — the textbook ring-back. ζ well below 1.
Round two — sharper edges
04
A pitch sneaks up through the air. No mechanical contact.
Reveal
Overdamped
Theremin — capacitive coupling, no mechanical inertia, glide rate set by hand motion.
No instrument-side dynamics to overshoot; the hand is the damping. Almost everything from a theremin sounds this way.
05
An acoustic guitar bend, released with discipline.
Reveal
Critically damped
Guitar — bend released with discipline, string tension settles fast.
Tempting to call underdamped — guitar bends often ring — but a controlled release brings tension home with no swing past. The finger is doing the damping work.
06
An electric guitar — fast bend, then finger vibrato.
Reveal
Underdamped
Electric guitar — fast bend, string tension overshoots, finger relaxes back.
Speed is what makes this underdamped: a slow bend would let the player ride the tension home; a fast one outruns the correction and overshoots. Vibrato keeps the ringing visible.
If you got 5 or 6 right — you have the regime ear. Next session: take the paired exercises to drill the physical-system guess.

If you got 3 or 4 right — re-listen to the misses with the reveal open. The overdamped/critically-damped boundary is the hardest: both lack ring, and only the shape of the approach separates them.

If you got 0–2 right — start over with eyes on the reveals. Knowing the answer before listening trains the regime onto the sound; surprise comes back after two passes.
