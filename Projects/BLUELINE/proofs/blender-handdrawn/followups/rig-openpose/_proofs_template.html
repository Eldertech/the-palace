Visual proofs · the MPFB2 native rebuild

From a pose to gen-AI ink one rig → three aligned conditioning plates → an inked figure

Figure Rig stages a 3D human and hands the gen-AI everything it needs to redraw that human in the locked pen-flow ink — the pose held, the legs two clean limbs, the body type dial-able. This page is the picture-proof of the rebuild: every stage, shown.

● The process, end to end

Four stages, one shared camera. The body and its skeleton are grown together (no force-fit); the three plates all register because they come off the same lens; the redraw anchors on all three at once.

● Registration — does the skeleton land on the body?

The OpenPose keypoints are read straight from the rig's own bone heads, so they sit exactly where the limbs are. Overlaid on the ink line: shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, and the face keypoints all land. 18 / 18 visible.

OpenPose ⊕ ink. Skeleton traced over the figure — both legs followed down to the ankles, no drift.

  • Skeleton = skin. 163-bone rig built for this mesh; OpenPose read from its bones.

◇ Variation — one flag flips the body

Male / female / young / old is a single macro dict — {gender, age, muscle, weight, height} — exposed as CLI flags. Same pipeline, same OpenPose remap, distinct bodies. Below: --gender 0.0 vs --gender 1.0.

Male ↔ female, same stride. Note the broader male shoulders vs the narrower female shoulders / wider hips — and the OpenPose registering on each.
