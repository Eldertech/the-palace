THE TARGET · locked pen-flow

One house style, held constant.

Everything here is measured against the style you locked by eye on 2026·06·19. It reads verbatim from locked-style.json:

modern pen and ink illustration, loose gestural linework, bold dramatic sweeping flow lines and speed streaks, scattered energetic ink blobs and splatter, stark high-contrast black and white, film-noir deep shadow, rough white paper, lots of white space, dynamic foreshortening, low canted angle.
negative · color · photoreal · 3d render · smooth digital shading · gradient

The negative is the whole game: it names a clean 3D render as the enemy. So Blender's job is never to look finished — it's to give honest structure, then get roughed up. Lineage you named: Inoue · sumi-e · noir.

PART ONE · Blender draws ink

Stills — line and value, in seconds.

Freestyle "sketchy ink"

The win is the waver. Stacked noise modifiers make the pen wobble; a calligraphy modifier gives pressure; sketchy chaining doubles strokes the way a hand does.

chaining SKETCHY·3 geo spatial+perlin noise thickness calligraphy 0.6–4.5

EEVEE toon ink-wash

Shader-to-RGB into a 2-band constant ramp = flat spotted blacks / paper white, with a stormy noir sky.

ramp CONSTANT · ink/paper gotcha view-transform = Standard

AgX (Blender 5 default) greys the paper — force Standard.

PART TWO · hand-drawn motion

Water, clouds, smoke — the ink breathes.

All procedural — no Mantaflow bake — so they re-render identically. Every frame is a pure function of the playhead, so they sync to the song. (muted · looping)

The boil. A per-frame handler re-seeds the line noise "on twos" so the ink wavers frame-to-frame like traditional animation, instead of sitting frozen. There's a clean OFF switch back to a steady line.

PART THREE · Blender → img2img → pen-flow

The seam is a redraw, not a filter.

Low denoise is a trap — SDXL just reconstructs your 3D render. You reach the locked ink only when you let the model redraw the medium: high denoise with Canny loosened to ~0.4 so structure only guides. The Blender render becomes a composition seed, not an appearance.

SettingVerdict
denoise 0.72 · canny 0.85● reconstructs render
denoise 0.90 · canny 0.55◐ half-toon
denoise 0.95 · canny 0.42● reaches pen-flow

Two hedges, both in the field notes: Canny alone won't hold a figure through a 0.95 redraw — a body needs pose/depth anchors. And SDXL-base reaches it inconsistently; the tuned RunPod ink checkpoint would hit it reliably at lower denoise, keeping more of the staging.

THE BREAKTHROUGH · splatter that lives in space

Ink blobs in three dimensions.

What if the splatter isn't a flat 2D effect — but ink suspended in the scene, so it parallaxes when the camera moves? It can be. (muted · looping)

  1. Analyze — find every ink mark in a pen-flow frame, separate blobs from linework, measure their character.
  2. Recreate — synthesize crisp hi-res blobs to that signature: round body + satellites + a thrown spatter tail.
  3. Place — scatter as camera-facing alpha billboards across a depth shell, excluded from Freestyle.
median Ø ~7px circularity ~1.0 · spiky tail 0.42 alpha blended · no depth-write (lines stay)

Billboards, not 3D geometry — a real ink-glob seen off-axis looks like a ball and breaks the flat-ink read. A camera-facing card keeps the mark flat drawn ink while giving it a 3D position. Flatness and parallax at once.

FOR BLUELINE · what to do with it

Four moves.

  1. Make Freestyle/toon a staging surface. It already emits most of pen-flow — line, value, exact composition — in ~2 s/frame. Add a "draft ink" pass to the Frame Designer staging leg, beside pose / depth / canny.
  2. Treat the seam as a redraw. Feed the Blender render as init at denoise ≥0.9 + loose Canny + pose/depth anchors, on the tuned ink model — to seed composition, not to be preserved.
  3. Hand-drawn motion = atmosphere behind figures. Water is the immediate keeper; clouds are easy; fire wants a small Mantaflow bake through the same ramp. Render with the boil; it stays song-synced.
  4. Keep it local for proofs, RunPod for the press. Stills stylize fine on this Mac (~2 min); motion stylization is too slow per frame locally — that belongs on the GPU backend.

Open question for you · is the Blender Freestyle ink itself close enough to ship as a register — cleaner, more controllable, but missing the organic blob texture — or strictly a seed for the gen-AI ink pass?
