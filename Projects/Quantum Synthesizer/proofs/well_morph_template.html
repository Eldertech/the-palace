Bend the well, and the tone becomes a bell

One solver, one potential that slowly bends from a parabola to a steeper quartic wall. At every step the Schrödinger equation is solved again, and the gaps between its energy levels are played as partials. Press play: what you hear and what you see are the same numbers.

V(x) = ½ · x² + λ · x⁴
(well height at x) = ½ × (position)² + (bend) × (position)⁴
fₙ = 196 Hz × (Eₙ − E₀) / (E₁ − E₀)
(nth partial) = (G3) × (level n − ground) ÷ (level 1 − ground)

The first strike is the parabolic well: level gaps equal, partials land exactly on the harmonic series, a clean G3. The last strike is the quartic-bent well from the earlier A/B proof: the walls climb faster than a parabola, so the upper levels spread apart and every partial above the first rides sharp. Nothing between them changes except V(x). Pause, then drag the slider to bend the well by hand and strike it.
