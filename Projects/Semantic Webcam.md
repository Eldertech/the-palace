# Semantic Webcam

![[Semantic Webcam — hero.png]]

A live camera that renders you as typeset language. Your luminance chooses the
character in each cell of a fixed grid; whole words typeset themselves into your
mid-tones; your brightest highlights become white tiles with black letters punched
into them. The picture is drawn in the white space of the letters. It runs entirely
in-page from your webcam — nothing is recorded.

## The line of iterations

The project *is* its build history — proof of the [[Oblique Enrichment]] thesis
that a card is a door. It began as one facet (the [[Synthesis ↔ Emergence]] flock)
and we could not stop:

- **Live STIGMERGY Cam** ([[01-live-stigmergy-cam.html]]) — the camera as an amber
  CP437 terminal grid; your motion lights the cells. An homage to the stigmergic
  board [[BBS Blackboard]]: traces, not orders — see [[Cooperation Yields Agency]].
- **Word Flock Cam** ([[02-word-flock-cam.html]]) — words as boids, flocking to
  detected eyes and fingertips. A branch set aside on the pivot to multiply, worth
  reviving.
- **Character Multiply Cam** ([[03-character-multiply-cam.html]]) *(the anchor)* —
  camera luminance × character ink; brightness picks the glyph (period → dash →
  your words → shade → white tile); whole words best-fit into the contiguous
  mid-tone runs and slide to hug each shape's left edge; highlights inverted to
  black-on-white. Temporal smoothing settles the words near-still.

## How it works

Downsample the webcam to a cell grid → per-cell luminance (with contrast + temporal
smoothing) → a tonal ramp where the *character itself* carries the value: a period
in shadow, a dash above it, the chosen **word-letter** through the mid-tones, heavy
shade, then a white tile. Words are typeset into each contiguous mid-tone run —
whole words only, best-fit from a per-source bank, left-aligned to the run. The
camera-luminance × character-ink multiply is literally a [[Convolution]]-flavored
blend of two character layers.

## Why it belongs to Oblique Enrichment

It is the first *productive output* of an oblique card — the flock provocation that
refused to stay a provocation. The natural endgame is to fold its own facets back
into the Enrichment server as iterable cards, closing the loop.

## Cross-Domain Resonance

- [[Oblique Portrait]] — a portrait made truer by refusing to render the face
  directly; here, by rendering it as words.
- [[Convolution]] — two layers multiplied into one image.

## Lost branches

- The eyes/fingers word-flock cam, parked at the multiply pivot.
- "Poetry that fits the blobs" — choosing words by grammar/markov so each tonal
  region reads as a composed line, not just length-fit.
- True-white vs warm-white highlights toggle.
- The unnamed concept underneath all of this: **language as a tonal medium /
  legibility as a band of light.** Possibly its own future entry.
