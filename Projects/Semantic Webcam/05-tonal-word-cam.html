Light picksthe word.

Iter 04 made each blob a composed line (a Markov walk, grammar not bag-of-words). Iter 05 asks the question the project carries: is language a tonal medium? So here brightness no longer only picks the glyph tier — it now weights the word choice itself. A bright blob prefers heavy, ink-dense, longer words; a dim blob prefers short, light, airy ones. Same grammar walk underneath, but every candidate is scored by how close its ink-weight sits to the blob's local luminance. Press w to A/B grammar → tonal, and b to sweep the bias from off to extreme.
