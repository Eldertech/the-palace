Characteristic multipliers ρ₁, ρ₂ in the complex plane

The two multipliers always satisfy ρ₁·ρ₂ = 1 (because the Mathieu equation is Hamiltonian). Two regimes: either they're a complex-conjugate pair on the unit circle (BOUNDED — system is stable, oscillating); or they're two real reciprocals, one inside / one outside the circle (UNBOUNDED — we are inside a tongue). The boundary is the moment the pair on the circle collides on the real axis at ρ = ±1 and splits into the real-reciprocal pair. (See media-08 for the static side-by-side and [[Floquet Theory]] for the deeper treatment.)
