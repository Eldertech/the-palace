Stability is decided by the characteristic multiplier ρ = eμT, where μ is the Floquet exponent and T = π is the modulation period. |ρ| ≤ 1 means the solution is bounded; |ρ| > 1 means it grows exponentially — you are inside a tongue. The cross-domain twin is Bloch's theorem in space: see [[Bloch ↔ Floquet Duality]] (media-11).
