Kapitza Inverted Pendulum

An inverted pendulum is unstable — but if you shake the pivot fast enough, the inverted position becomes stable. The pencil stays balanced on its tip. This is Floquet engineering of stability — modulation alone, no measurement, no feedback.

pendulum on a vertically-oscillating pivot

Equation of motion in the lab frame: θ̈ + (g/L − ap·Ω²/L · cos(Ωt)) · sin θ = 0. At fast pivot oscillation, the average dynamics around the inverted equilibrium produces an effective restoring potential that points up rather than down. Above the Kapitza threshold (apΩ)² > 2gL, the inverted position is a STABLE equilibrium. In the Strutt diagram, this is the negative-a Kapitza-stable region. (See [[Floquet Theory]] and media-05.)
