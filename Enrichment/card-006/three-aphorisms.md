# THREE APHORISMS FOR THE BESSEL FUNCTION

*An offering for [[Bessel Functions in Synthesis]] — one couplet per substrate, the same function answering three different questions.*

---

**I.  *FM synthesis.***
The carrier asks the modulator: *how loud should I be?*
The Bessel function answers: *that depends on how hard you push.*

**II.  *The drum head.***
The membrane asks: *where can I stand still while still being struck?*
The Bessel function answers: *at every zero you can name.*

**III.  *The particle in the cylinder.***
The wavefunction asks: *where am I allowed to live?*
The Bessel function answers: *where I am, and only where I am.*

---

*One function. Three substrates. The instrument always asks an old question dressed in new physics.*
