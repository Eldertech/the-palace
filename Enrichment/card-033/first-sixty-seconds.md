*The tutorial's first 60 seconds. Loudon addresses a student who has never heard a Shepard tone:*

"Before I play anything, I want you to notice what you're assuming. You think pitch is a line — lower on the left, higher on the right, going somewhere when it moves. That assumption is about to break.

Here's what's going to happen: I'm going to play a sequence of notes that keeps going up. It will feel like it's always ascending. And it will never arrive anywhere. You'll keep waiting to hit the ceiling — and the ceiling never comes.

Don't try to figure it out while you're listening. Just let it be strange. The explanation can wait. The strangeness has to happen first, or the explanation lands on nothing.

Ready? Listen."

*[First Shepard tone plays.]*

"See that? That's the illusion working. You heard it going up. It is going up. And it's also going nowhere. Both of those things are true at the same time. What you just felt is your auditory system making a choice — it heard the local motion and missed the global circle. That choice is the thing we're building. Let's talk about how to make it happen."
