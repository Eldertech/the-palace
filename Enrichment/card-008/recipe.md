# GENERATIVE SAMPLE LIBRARY — A RECIPE

*serves: one playable bank, approximately 2,400 samples*
*prep time: one afternoon for the corpus; one evening for the routing*

---

## INGREDIENTS

- **1 distinct timbral source** — your mouth, your instrument, a field recording, a single synthesizer patch in one mood
- **4 cups intended utterances** — syllables, hits, phonemes, short events; any unit shorter than the patience of the listener
- **1 routing layer** — Kokoro, an AI sub-agent, Claude conducting the interview, or the engineer's intuition
- **2 tablespoons forward-onset metadata** per sample
- **A pinch of stylistic constraint** — do not reach beyond the source's natural envelope, no matter what your routing layer suggests
- **A small open sampler format**, ready

---

## INSTRUCTIONS

1. **Record the corpus in one session.** One signal chain. One tempo of mind. Cooking the bank from many sessions produces a less coherent stew. The autobiography of the source is the seasoning; do not dilute it.

2. **Hand the corpus to your routing layer.** Ask: *what 2,400 utterances would this voice make if it were sampling itself?* Not *what does the genre want.* Not *what would be useful.* The question is what the voice would do.

3. **Cull aggressively.** A ratio of one used utterance to three discarded is healthy. Keeping the discarded ones in a side jar is a sign of weakness; release them to the compost.

4. **Equalize onset.** 5 ms tolerance, per-file responsive-onset pipeline. Set the `lead-in` metadata field on every sample. This is the step most often skipped and most often missed later.

5. **Plate in the sampler of choice.** Sealed, the bank will keep five years before re-rolling becomes interesting; if you re-rolled it sooner, you cooked too long the first time.

---

## TROUBLESHOOTING

- **If the bank tastes "small"** — you are over-cleaning. Leave more of the speaker's natural texture. Roughness is not noise; it is voice.
- **If the bank tastes "muddled"** — your routing layer is doing too much. Reduce the prompt. The routing layer is a mandolin, not a stockpot.
- **If the bank tastes "boring"** — your corpus was not cooked in one mood. Re-record under a single weather.
- **If the bank surprises you** — taste again before adjusting. Surprise is the goal, not the symptom.

---

## A NOTE ON SIZE

A 200-sample bank that the routing layer can extend on request beats a 5,000-sample one-shot bank that pretends to cover the territory. *A sample bank is a small generative library, not a large one-shot bank.* This is the only rule that is not negotiable.

---

## WINE PAIRING

- Pair a *phoneme-bank* with a track that wants spine.
- Pair a *melodic-bank* with arrangement that wants a soloist who shows up briefly and leaves.
- Pair a *texture-bank* with a piece that has been hesitant to commit. Texture-banks make decisions for the timid composer.

---

*The kitchen is your studio. The corpus is the day. The bank is what you serve.*
