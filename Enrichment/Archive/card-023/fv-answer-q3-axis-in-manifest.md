*Proposal (2026-05-05):* The specialist-manifest pattern surfaced in the maker-architecture work gives us a place to record this without corrupting the compression itself. A specialist (atomic maker) declares its preferred axis on its manifest — "I optimize for high perception on briefs about X; I optimize for low distortion on briefs about Y." The Producer's curated options carry the manifest's axis-tag forward as metadata; the Director sees axis priorities as part of the {artifact, limits, alternatives} triplet rather than as annotations bolted onto deposited entries. The compression stays clean; the meta-axis lives in the manifest layer that produced it. See [[Palace Agent Infrastructure Spec]] §3.1 for the manifest concept this generalizes.
