# Listening Exercise: The Three Damping Regimes

**Duration:** 8 minutes. **Materials:** Headphones. A human voice.

---

## Setup

Before you play anything, slide a finger slowly across your own forearm from wrist to elbow. That's overdamped — arrival without overshoot, languid and certain.

Now flick your fingernail against your thumbnail. The sting and ring before it resolves: underdamped.

You've already heard the difference. Now we name it.

---

## Exercise 1 — Overdamped: The Surrender (2 minutes)

Hum a note. Slide to a note a minor third lower. Don't stop partway. Don't ornament. Just let go.

Listen for the **absence of arrival** — the voice doesn't land so much as it dissolves into the new pitch. There's no moment of "click." It comes to rest like a door closing on a thick carpet.

This is ζ > 1. Friction wins. The system never revisits where it came from.

---

## Exercise 2 — Underdamped: The Overshoot (3 minutes)

Sing up to a high note from a perfect fifth below — and *arrive sharp*. Land a quarter-tone above the target and drift down into it. Let the resolution be audible.

Now sing it again and notice: **you probably did this naturally the first time.** The larynx overshoots. This is not a mistake. This is the body's inertia.

Listen for the overshoot as a feature, not an error. The note has life because it fought to arrive.

This is 0 < ζ < 1. The resonance rings past the target and rings back.

---

## Exercise 3 — Critically Damped: The Purposeful Arrival (2 minutes)

This one is hardest. Slide to a note — but make the slide feel *decisive*. No overshoot. No languid dissolve. A single clean arrival, as fast as possible without ringing.

This lives between the other two. Most humans can't do it with voice because the larynx has its own ζ baked in. Trying to hit it reveals the constraint.

When you hear a plucked guitar note that settles immediately with no drift, no ring: that's critical damping.

---

## What to listen for after

When you hear a vocalist next, ask: *is this person overshooting?* (Underdamped — energetic, alive, slightly sharp on arrival.) *Or surrendering?* (Overdamped — warm, liquid, unhurried.) The three regimes are everywhere once you have names for them.

The names are not metaphors. They are the physics.
