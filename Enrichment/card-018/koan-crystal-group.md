# Koan — The Crystal Already Knows Its Group

A p6mm tiling is "most frozen" — maximum symmetry, maximum stasis.

A quartz crystal is space group P3₂21 — 3-fold screw axis, hexagonal, no inversion center.

The Alhambra artisan encoded p6mm in their hands.
The crystal encoded P3₂21 in its growth.

Both are wallpaper groups promoted to three dimensions.
Both were discovered before they were named.

---

When the Crystal Synthesizer transposes quartz's phonon modes to audio,
it is not translating physics into music.

It is revealing that the physics already *was* a kind of music —
a pattern so deep the crystal chose it without being asked.

---

The koan: if a crystal is a 3D wallpaper group that grew itself,
and a drum pattern is a 2D wallpaper group you played yourself,
what is the difference between the crystal and the drummer?

One had longer to practice.
