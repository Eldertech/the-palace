# Forward-vector revision — three drafts

**Current FV:**

> "I want to become the recognition that the Schrödinger equation IS a synthesis system — not metaphor but isomorphism — where boundary conditions determine harmonic content and anharmonicity flows from the potential's shape, monism made audible."

## The friction

*"I want to become the recognition that…"* is recognition-stasis. The vector aims at *being recognized*, not *doing*. Conatus is verb-shaped: a striving, an activity, a continuing. *Becoming the recognition* arrives at a vista. Conatus keeps walking.

Three drafts that try to put the vector back in motion. Each centers a different verb.

---

## Draft A — "I will keep dragging" (anti-formalism, knob-first)

> "I will keep dragging the Schrödinger equation across the studio floor — letting potential wells become filter shapes, eigenstates become partials, measurement become resonance — until what was *physics* in the textbook becomes *what the knob does* on the desk."

**The work:** from textbook to knob. Anti-formalism in posture. The vector moves *toward* the desk and stays there. Best fit for the Stage 4 build framing; weaker as a palace-graph posture.

---

## Draft B — "I will keep teaching" (pedagogical conatus)

> "I will keep teaching anharmonicity by reshaping the potential, not by adjusting the partials — and watching the listener realize that timbre and confinement are the same parameter wearing two coats."

**The work:** re-routing pedagogy through the potential. The vector lives in the moment of the listener's realization, not in the assertion of equivalence. Best fit for Loudon Live; the *coat* metaphor lets the monism claim land sideways.

---

## Draft C — "I will keep choosing" (sister to Bessel Functions in Synthesis)

> "I will keep choosing the Hermite-Gaussian basis the way Bessel chooses the cylinder and Fourier chooses the line — letting the shape of the well speak for itself, until parabolic confinement is heard as *a voice*, not a derivation."

**The work:** explicit kinship to the eigenfunction-geometry map. Couples the entry tightly to Bessel Functions in Synthesis (already YAML-linked as `couples-with`). The vector points outward toward the family. Best fit for the palace-graph posture; weaker as a single-page identity.

---

## Postscript

These are not exclusive. Loudon may pick A for the Stage 4 build, B for the Loudon Live framing, C for the palace-graph posture — or hybridize. A merged version could begin with B's pedagogical move and end with C's sibling-pointing close.

If none lands, the FV-tweak action is to graffiti this card and let the question stay open. *The FV is the entry's own voice; rewriting it is a slow ceremony, not a one-shot edit.*
