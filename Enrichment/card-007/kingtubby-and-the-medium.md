# KING TUBBY'S GHOST AND THE SÉANCE MEDIUM

*A short voice-act for [[Retrospective Delay]]'s Stage 4 mythology — the dub engineer and his channel, two characters who can voice the JSUI theatrical layer.*

---

**The medium**, hands above the crystal ball, eyes half-closed:
*Is anyone there.*

**Tubby's ghost**, faintly, from inside the ball:
*Mostly. Pull it back four bars.*

**Medium:**
*That's not how this works.*

**Tubby:**
*That's the only way it works. Anything you hear now happened. The board you're holding — that round one with the blue glow — what does it do?*

**Medium:**
*It receives.*

**Tubby:**
*Wrong. It re-receives. The first reception is the recording. Yours is the séance. Pull it back four bars. Fade in the snare. Now hold the snare while the bass plays again — yes, the bass that already played, that one. Hold the snare on top of it. Don't tell me you can't. Watch.*

**Medium**, holding her hand still:
*…there it is.*

**Tubby:**
*That. That moment. That is where dub lives. Not in the studio. In the gap between the first time you heard it and the second. The studio was just where I learned the trick.*

**Medium:**
*And what am I, then?*

**Tubby:**
*The room where it happens again. You don't make sounds. You give old sounds a second chance to be new ones.*

---

*Notes for the JSUI build: the medium's gestures map to the four-bar lookback windows. The crystal ball is the mixing-board surface. Tubby's voice can be a low-passed dry recitation; her voice can be heavily verbed and reverse-attacked. Stage 4's first user moment is hearing them disagree about whether the bass already played.*
