# Suno Prompt — The Sound of a Delay That Thinks

**Title:** *Physarum* (The Routing Learns)

**Style prompt:**
ambient, generative, evolving texture, sparse, wet delay, organic, field recording adjacent, no drums, no melody, slow emergence, feedback topology, droning

**Lyrics / descriptive prompt:**
No lyrics. Pure process sound.

A single sustained note — maybe a prepared piano tap, maybe a sine-wave pluck — enters into silence. Its delay tail appears once, then twice, then three times at slightly different intervals, as if the system is searching. For thirty seconds nothing changes. Then a second source enters — a low bowed string or a distant bell tone — and the delay system begins reconfiguring. The two signals' delay tails reach toward each other, interleave briefly, then separate. The tail of the first note thickens; the tail of the second grows quieter. By the two-minute mark the routing has stabilized: the original source is fuller, more present; the second source echoes at a longer delay-time from a different angle. Everything sounds deliberate. Nothing was designed.

Slowly, a new delay tap appears — a ghost arrival from a direction the two established paths hadn't found yet. The routing is not finished.

**Mood target:** the feeling of watching a vine grow across a surface and deciding it is intelligent. Not eerie. Not mechanical. *Patient.*

**Duration:** 3–4 minutes
