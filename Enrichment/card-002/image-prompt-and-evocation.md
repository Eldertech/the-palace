# SHIMMER CLOUD — image prompt + one-line evocation

*An offering for [[Shimmer Cloud]], a spore that has not yet been seen.*

---

## ONE-LINE EVOCATION

*A reverb is a room remembering you; a shimmer is the room recalling everyone you have been.*

— could sit as the first line of the entry's body, before any technical description, as a hook.

---

## IMAGE PROMPT (Midjourney / Flux / SDXL)

```
A horizontal cross-section through a thunderhead at dusk, but the cloud is
made of glass dust — billions of micro-prisms suspended in moisture. White
light enters from one edge and emerges from the opposite edge dispersed
into thirty-two pitch-shifted ghost beams that fan outward and overlap into
a soft chord of color. Inside the cloud: caustics, voronoi patterns, faint
spectral bands like a slowly-moving prism field. The cloud's interior is
sub-surface lit, glowing as though from within. Background is a vertical
gradient of deep teal into magenta. No people, no buildings, no horizon —
the viewer is inside the cloud, looking out toward the dispersed exit.
Photoreal at the edge of physical possibility. 16:9. Cinematic, painterly.
--ar 16:9 --style raw --s 250
```

## A SECOND PROMPT, MORE ABSTRACT (alternate)

```
An interior view of a granular cloud rendered as a chord of pitch-shifted
glass: 64 floating crystal grains, each refracting the same incoming sine
wave at a different transposition, suspended in dark space lit only by
their own internal caustics. Long depth of field, near-grains pin-sharp,
distant grains soft. The composition implies a vast spatial texture but
fits in a single frame. Mood: contemplative, hyperreal, late-Tarkovsky.
--ar 16:9 --s 200
```

---

## NOTES FOR THE SPORE

The prompts are intentionally without "music," "audio," or "synthesizer" in them. The image you want is one that holds the *spatial logic of pitch-shifted dispersion* visually — a viewer who has never used a shimmer reverb should look at the image and understand, without being told, what the instrument's atmosphere feels like. If the image works, it works because the visual phenomenon and the auditory phenomenon are the same phenomenon at different speeds (per [[Frequency-Time Duality]]).

If neither prompt feels right, the failure mode worth noticing is which: too literal (glass dust is too much craft and not enough mood) or too abstract (the second prompt may not generate the prism logic that's the whole point). The next iteration steers from there.
