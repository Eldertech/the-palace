## How to Use This Library

**For research:** Cross-reference a palace entry's "born" date and "pillars" with this library. What was Loudon reading when the entry emerged?

**For idea-seeding:** When stuck on a palace entry, check what sources connect to it. Often a re-engagement with source material sparks new connections.

**For teaching:** Many entries here are touchstones for students. Referencing *GEB*, Spinoza, or Feynman provides context for why the palace is structured as it is.

**For growth:** Entries marked "unread" or "referenced-but-not-engaged" are invitations. Deeper engagement with these sources will likely spawn new palace entries or reveal hidden connections.
