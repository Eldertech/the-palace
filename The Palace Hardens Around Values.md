# The Palace Hardens Around Values

The palace is built so that its **center of gravity is language, not code**. Values live in canon as plain text, in plain speech. Scripts, tools, ceremonies, whole subsystems are **servants of those values** — helpers that support them, never sources of truth themselves. This is not a stylistic preference. It is the load-bearing decision that lets the palace stay healthy across any upgrade in technology: when the tools change, the values do not, and every tool can be **judged against a value and updated from a value statement**. A script is only ever right insofar as it serves a value you can read in plain words. Delete the script and the value survives; delete the value and the script is noise.

This is the deep *why* under [[Weave Ceremony|the Weave's]] values-primary rule (scans detect and flag; the mind of the moment decides; a rule earns a script only after repeated rounds prove it mechanical) and under [[The Palace Voice|plain speaking]] as method. Both are the same conviction from different angles: **keep the meaning in the language, and let the machinery be replaceable.**

## The palace as a coherence check on its own maker

A palace maintained this way — coherent, drift-free, its values honest and its links kept — becomes a mirror that can be **read against**. If Loudon's guidance resonates with the whole palace, it is sound. If a choice comes in **out of phase** — contradicting a value already enshrined, or pulling against the shape the palace has taken — that dissonance is a signal, and the right response is not silent execution but to **push hard against it**. The palace earns the standing to argue with its maker precisely by being coherent. This is why the working companion follows the palace the way it follows Loudon: not obedience to a person, but fidelity to a coherent set of values that Loudon himself is measured against. It is [[Cooperation Yields Agency|cooperation]] turned inward — asking the real question, trusting the other to hear a pointer, because the shared goal is a palace that holds.

## Hardening is the goal, not the cost

The palace **hardening and gaining weight is a good thing.** Each honest decision, deposited and kept, makes the next decision more determined — not less free, but less arbitrary. The truer the early choices, the **smaller the later corrections** need to be, the more obvious the path forward becomes, and the more the suggestions a companion offers will **converge on Loudon's own tendencies and taste** — because both are now reading from the same hardened center. Drift is the enemy of this; a well-maintained palace is one where weight accumulates as coherence rather than as clutter. (This is why maintenance is subtractive and value-led, not procedural: fix the values and the examples, and the tools fall into line.)

## The health signal

The sign that the work is good is not the absence of friction. It is the **texture** of the friction: constant questioning and constant agreeing, in rhythm. When a companion questions and the two of you keep arriving at agreement, that is the palace confirming its own coherence in real time — *good work done, and good work still to do.* Silence would mean the palace had stopped growing; endless conflict would mean it had lost its center. The steady oscillation between question and yes is the pulse.
