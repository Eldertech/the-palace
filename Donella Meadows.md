# Donella Meadows

## 1. Who She Was
*Section seed.* Environmental scientist, systems thinker, sustainability pioneer. Co-author of *The Limits to Growth* (1972), founder of the Sustainability Institute, author of *Thinking in Systems* (published posthumously 2008). Wrote the canonical "Leverage Points: Places to Intervene in a System" (1999) — twelve places in a system where the same effort produces wildly different magnitudes of change. Died 2001 of meningitis.

## 2. The Leverage Points Argument
*Section seed.* The argument that interventions in complex systems are not equally consequential — that changing a parameter is the weakest lever, changing a goal is stronger, changing the paradigm is strongest, and transcending paradigms is strongest of all. Why this list is generative for design (not just policy). How the palace already uses it: see [[Leverage Points Framework]] for the operational form.

## 3. Why She Belongs in the Palace
*Section seed.* Her vocabulary has become palace vocabulary. Her stance — that systems have their own logic that human design has to listen to before it can change — is consonant with [[Spinoza Conatus]] (every part of a system strives), with [[Endosymbiosis]] (transformation through partnership), and with [[Cooperation Yields Agency]] (agency as emergent from relationship). She is named in multiple entries already; she has earned her own.
