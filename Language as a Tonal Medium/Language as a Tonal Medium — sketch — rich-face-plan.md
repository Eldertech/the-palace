# A rich-face plan for Language as a Tonal Medium

The five iterations of [[Semantic Webcam]] are the evidence this entry's § *The evidence — five iterations of one instrument* describes in prose. Each is a single playable HTML already in `Projects/Semantic Webcam/`, and each proves one step. Laid beside that section on the entry's rich face ([[Enrichment]]), they let a reader put their own face in front of the claim.

| piece | the step it proves |
|---|---|
| `01-live-stigmergy-cam.html` | Iteration zero: glyphs as pure tone, nothing reads yet — feel the meaning channel absent before it locks in. |
| `02-word-flock-cam.html` | Meaning present but unmoored from tone: words that flock and say something but refuse to shade — the failure that proves the two channels must be joined on purpose. |
| `03-character-multiply-cam.html` | The anchor: a region that is the right darkness *and* a readable word at once, a convolution-flavored multiply you drive. |
| `04-grammar-fit-cam.html` | The strong test: words picked by grammar, tonal fit the only hard constraint — does sense survive? The page's open question, free-rider meaning or a separate budget, in the reader's hands. |
| `05-tonal-word-cam.html` | The settling: smoothing holds the words near-still, so tone becomes legible and legibility becomes the texture of the tone. |

All five need a camera; the rich face should say so before the first one asks.
