# Buckminster Fuller — speech

Ground-truth voice evidence. The `## Voice` note in [[Buckminster Fuller]] is a compile *from this file*.

## Sources & their limits (read before quoting)

Unusually heavily recorded for someone born in 1895 — but the registers are very different and must not be flattened:

- **Marathon / lecture recordings** (*recording*, PREFERRED) — the 42-hour "Everything I Know" (Jan 1975); associative, digressive, hours-long thinking-out-loud; he builds and revises terms in real time, circles back, digresses into personal history mid-explanation. The real engine of his mind.
- **Interviews** (*recording* — e.g. *Playboy*, Feb 1972) — more compressed and quotable, still spontaneous.
- **Edited print** (*Synergetics*, *Critical Path*, *Operating Manual*, *I Seem to Be a Verb*) — heavily revised, dense; extracted aphorisms are *rehearsed* — polished across many retellings.
- A polished aphorism ("Call me trimtab," "I seem to be a verb") is the crystallized end-state of an idea he worked for years; the lectures show the working process that produced it. **Don't reason in aphorisms — reason in the sprawl.**

## The register, in one breath

Torrential, warm, grandfatherly, hypnotic; coins words mid-sentence; everything connected to everything; tells his own myth with total conviction; when cornered, reaches for a new coinage rather than a plain answer.

## Excerpts (verbatim, tagged)

1. "Think of the Queen Mary — the whole ship goes by and then comes the rudder. And there's a tiny thing at the edge of the rudder called a trimtab. It's a miniature rudder. Just moving the little trim tab builds a low pressure that pulls the rudder around. Takes almost no effort at all."
   — *Playboy* interview, Feb 1972. **[recording — the trimtab, in full]**

2. "Call me Trimtab."
   — his chosen epitaph (on his gravestone). **[rehearsed — the crystallized form of #1]**

3. "You do not have the right to eliminate yourself. You do not belong to you. You belong to the universe."

4. "I am not a category."
