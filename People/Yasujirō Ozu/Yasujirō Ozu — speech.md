# Yasujirō Ozu — speech

Ground-truth voice evidence. The `## Voice` note in [[Yasujirō Ozu]] is a compile *from this file*.

## Sources & their limits (READ THIS — it is load-bearing)

- **His primary "voice" is his films and method, not his talk.** Few interviews survive in accessible English form; what circulates is overwhelmingly *secondary compilation* (aggregator sites) repeating a handful of lines with no dated original interview/outlet.
- **The famous "tofu" line cannot be traced** to a specific primary interview/date in this research pass — it recurs across many independent secondary sources (so: real, not invented) but the paper trail is thin. **Attributed-but-provenance-thin.** The three renderings below (#1–3) are one remark retranslated, not three remarks.
- **Translation is a constant veil** — Japanese→English by mostly uncredited hands; word choice varies between renderings of the same underlying remark.
- **The diaries are the most reliable primary record** — but terse, logistical, weather-and-sake, *not* manifesto.
- **His register, where verifiable, is modest, wry, and deflecting** — he undercuts "philosophy"/"art" questions with craft-language. **NEVER write an expansive, theoretical, self-explaining Ozu. If a scene has him holding forth about mono no aware or transcendence, it is a forgery — catch it in review.**

## The register, in one breath

Understated, wry, craft-first; deflects grandeur into food and cutting; demonstrates rather than argues; falls silent rather than theorize.

4. "I consciously did away with fade-ins and replaced them with the cut… neither dissolve, fade-in nor fade-out can be regarded as 'the grammar of film,' they are no more than characteristics of the camera."

5. "Some people say that Ozu is a really obstinate buzzard." / "stubborn old buzzard."
   — **[attributed self-description · secondary]** — note the third-person self-reference; wry deflection, not self-explanation.

6. "The wide screen reminds me of a roll of toilet paper."
   — **[attributed · secondary · no dated primary]** — refusing CinemaScope as a joke, not a manifesto; the register in one line.

7. "I didn't make my films for critics, nor for public pleasure. I did it for myself."

8. Diary, on finishing the *Tokyo Story* script: "Finished. 103 days; 43 bottles of sake."
   — **[diary · the most reliably PRIMARY item here]** (Criterion's published diary excerpts). The character of the man: understated, deadpan, allergic to grand statement — and notably the least "quotable."
