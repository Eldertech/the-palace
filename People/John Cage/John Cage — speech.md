# John Cage — speech

Ground-truth evidence for how Cage actually speaks, for the entry's `## Voice` note and for deep casts. **Build the profile from these; two of his most-cited "sources" are not real speech at all — see below.** Verify exact wording against the primary source before quoting (WebFetch was unavailable when this was seeded).

## Sources & their limits (READ FIRST — this citizen has traps)

- **❌ The Ted Berrigan "Interview with John Cage" (1966, *Mother* no. 7) is a FABRICATION.** Berrigan invented it whole, as a Dadaist act; a panel (Sontag, Brustein; George Plimpton selecting) awarded it best interview of the year before realizing. Cage was amused. **Do not use it as evidence of his speech** — it measures the myth, not the man.
- **⚠️ Kostelanetz, *Conversing with Cage* is a Cage-*approved collage*** — excerpts from 150+ interviews across four decades, rearranged by topic, "checked and corrected by Cage himself." Excellent for his *phrasing and ideas*; **not** evidence of spontaneous conversational rhythm (it flattens era and setting).
- **Context by setting:** 1960 *I've Got a Secret* = performative TV (sly, comic timing); late-life interviews (Bruce Duffie; "Cage at 70/75") = closest to his real, gentle spontaneous voice; the *Silence* aphorisms = **composed literary prose**, not talk.

## Confirmed traits (multi-source, late-life accounts)

Voice **soft, higher-pitched, lilting, slightly sibilant**; **gentle, gracious, unhurried** — would "go on for the rest of the afternoon." **Laughs easily**, a grin bursting a weathered face. **Humble** to the point of self-effacement; "moves hesitantly"; a "quiet aura of Zen humility." Enigmatic on the surface, coherent underneath.

## Excerpts (verbatim where marked; each tagged by context)

- **[performative TV · 1960]** "I consider music the production of sound, and since in the piece which you will hear I produce sound, I will call it music." — correcting the host, placidly, with a small smile. His signature *reframe-the-question* move. (Hyperallergic, on *I've Got a Secret*.)
- **[interview · on audience laughter]** "Of course. I consider laughter preferable to tears." — said in his soft voice; he cited Kant that music and laughter are the two things that needn't mean anything to give deep pleasure. *verify exact wording/source.*
- **[interview · everyday/mushrooms]** his mushroom-hunting clothes as "a collection of odors I produce and gather while rambling in the woods." *verify.*
- **[Zen device · not everyday speech]** in the *Lecture on Nothing* Q&A he gave "six previously prepared answers regardless of the question asked" — a deliberate stunt; do not generalize it to conversation.
- **[LITERARY — NOT speech]** "I have nothing to say and I am saying it and that is poetry as I need it"; "There is no such thing as silence." — composed for the page (*Silence*, *Lecture on Nothing*). Keep separate from his talking voice.
