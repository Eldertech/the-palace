# Buckminster Fuller

![[Buckminster Fuller — hero.png]]
## Systems Thinker

**Domain:** Systems Thinker

---

## Key Works

- Operating Manual for Spaceship Earth (1969)
- Synergetics (1975)
- Critical Path (1981)

---

## Core Concepts

- **Synergetics** - study of systems in transformation
- **Ephemeralization** - doing more with less
- **Tensegrity** - tension + integrity structures
- **Spaceship Earth** - finite resources, crew perspective
- **Dymaxion principle** - maximum gain from minimal input

---

## Connection to 4 Pillars

Fuller's systems thinking applies directly to synthesis architecture, signal flow design, and efficient creative practice. His 'design science' approach mirrors building Max patches: find elegant solutions that do more with less. Synergetics = understanding how whole systems behave differently than parts predict.

---

## Key Quotes

> "You never change things by fighting the existing reality. To change something, build a new model that makes the existing model obsolete."
> "I just invent, then wait until man comes around to needing what I've invented."
> "Everyone is born a genius, but the process of living de-geniuses them."

Read Operating Manual cover to cover (short, profound). Apply to music production: What's your 'comprehensive anticipatory design science'? What can you ephemeralize in your workflow?
