# Natalie Goldberg
## Writer

**Domain:** Writer

---

## Key Works

- Writing Down the Bones (1986)
- Wild Mind (1990)
- The True Secret of Writing (2013)

---

## Core Concepts

- **Writing practice** - timed sessions, keep hand moving
- **First thoughts** - don't edit while creating
- **Composting** - let ideas sit, process unconsciously
- **Monkey mind** - working with distraction, not against it
- **Writing as Zen practice** - meditation through making

---

## Connection to 4 Pillars

Goldberg's 'writing practice' = jamming/improvising in music. Keep creating, don't edit mid-flow. Her 'first thoughts' = first takes. Her composting = letting tracks sit before mixing. Monkey mind = the inner critic during creation - acknowledge it, keep making anyway.

---

## Key Quotes

> "Write what disturbs you, what you fear, what you have not been willing to speak about. Be willing to be split open."
> "Whether you're keeping a journal or writing as a meditation, it's the same thing. What's important is you're having a relationship with your mind."
> "This is your life. You are responsible for it. You will not live forever. Don't wait."

---

## Recommended Reading Approach

Do her writing practice exercises as music practice: Set timer for 10 minutes, create continuously, no stopping/editing. Keep hand moving (keep sound coming). Apply to production sessions.

---

## Applications to Four Pillars

### Creation Pillar
Timed jam sessions - no stopping to edit. Capture first thoughts, refine later. Separate creation from editing.

### Tools Pillar
Keep the flow going. Record everything, curate later. Don't break creative flow for technical perfection.

### Philosophy Pillar
Practice as meditation. Monkey mind is normal - work with it. The point is showing up, not perfection.

### Practice Pillar
Daily 10-minute jams. Timer running, keep creating, no editing. Build fluency through volume.
