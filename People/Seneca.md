# Seneca
## Philosopher

**Domain:** Philosopher

---

## Key Works

- Letters from a Stoic (Moral Epistles to Lucilius)
- On the Shortness of Life
- On Providence

---

## Core Concepts

- **Time as most valuable resource** - use it well
- **Premeditation of adversity** - rehearse challenges
- **Memento mori** - remember you will die
- **Negative visualization** - imagine loss to appreciate presence
- Constant self-examination

---

## Connection to 4 Pillars

Seneca on time management applies directly to creative practice. 'It's not that we have little time, but that we waste much of it.' How much studio time lost to distraction? Premeditation: Before starting a project, imagine what could go wrong. Be ready. Memento mori: Finite time = urgency + presence.

---

## Key Quotes

> "It is not that we have a short time to live, but that we waste a lot of it."
> "We suffer more often in imagination than in reality."
> "True happiness is to enjoy the present, without anxious dependence upon the future."

---

## Recommended Reading Approach

Letters from a Stoic - read 1-2 letters per week. Short, practical, directly applicable. Keep a Seneca notebook: quote at top, your application below.

## Applications to Four Pillars

### Creation Pillar
Time consciousness in production. Every hour in the studio is precious - use it fully present.

### Tools Pillar
Premeditate technical failures. Hard drive crash? Backups ready. Software bug? Workaround prepared.

### Philosophy Pillar
Negative visualization: Imagine losing ability to make music. Appreciate it now.

### Practice Pillar
Daily time audit: Where did creative hours go? Wasted or invested?
