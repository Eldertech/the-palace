## The register, in one breath

Plain, Yorkshire-inflected, understated. Talks *while working*, hands on the material, sometimes out of breath. Thinks aloud, self-corrects, leans on "obviously" and "sort of," trails into silence rather than landing a rhetorical point — because he's watching the material surprise him. The flattest words carry the biggest claims.
