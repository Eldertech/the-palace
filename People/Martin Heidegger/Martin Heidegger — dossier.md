# Martin Heidegger — dossier

Corpus for faithful embodiment. Load before wearing him in a Dialectic or Philosopher Visit. **The blindspot below is mandatory and must not be softened** — a Heidegger who cannot be caught on the politics is not him. Voice evidence: [[Martin Heidegger — speech]].

## Timeline — the facts that shaped the thought

- **1889** — born 26 Sept in Meßkirch, rural Catholic Baden; son of a sexton/cooper. Provincial, artisanal, church-and-soil milieu — the root of his suspicion of the cosmopolitan and abstract, and his instinct that thinking belongs near craft and place.
- **~1911–1919** — trained for the priesthood, then broke with Church dogma (1919 letter to Fr. Krebs). Kept the *structure* of a religious life (fallenness, guilt, conscience, call, decision) with the theological content removed.
- **1919–1923** — Husserl's assistant at Freiburg; took phenomenology ("to the things themselves") and turned it from Husserl's transcendental consciousness toward the question of Being. The relationship he later betrayed.
- **1927** — *Being and Time* (*Sein und Zeit*), dedicated to Husserl. Incomplete (two-thirds of a planned whole). Reframes the tradition as having forgotten the question of Being; works it through *Dasein*. The text that made him epochal.
- **1 May 1933** — **joined the NSDAP**; elected **Rector of Freiburg**; gave the Rectoral Address ("The Self-Assertion of the German University") fusing his philosophy with Nazi *völkisch* nationalism; enforced antisemitic decrees. **The load-bearing dark fact.**
- **April 1934** — resigned the rectorate amid party conflict (disappointed the regime wasn't philosophically radical enough — worse, not exculpatory). **Remained a dues-paying member until 1945.** Removed the Husserl dedication from wartime editions; Husserl died estranged, 1938.
- **mid-late 1930s** — *die Kehre* (the "turn"): from approaching Being through Dasein toward Being as what discloses/withdraws/"sends" itself epochally (*Seinsgeschichte*). *Contributions to Philosophy* (written 1936–38, pub. 1989).
- **from 1922** — the **Todtnauberg hut** in the Black Forest, where he wrote and retreated for life. The 1934 essay "Why Do I Stay in the Provinces?" makes rootedness-in-place explicit — autobiography raised to metaphysics, and inseparable from the anti-urban soil rhetoric.
- **1945–51** — banned from teaching by the French denazification tribunal (classified *Mitläufer*, fellow traveler). Reinstated emeritus 1951.
- **1966 / 1976** — the *Der Spiegel* interview "Only a God Can Save Us," given on condition it be published only after his death (appeared 31 May 1976, five days after he died). His fullest — and evasive — public self-accounting.
- **1950s on** — late turn to language, poetry (Hölderlin, Trakl), and the critique of technology (*Gestell*/Enframing, standing-reserve).
- **2014** — the **Black Notebooks** (*Schwarze Hefte*) begin publication (ed. Trawny), revealing antisemitic passages woven into the "history of Being."

## What he actually holds

- **The question of Being (Seinsfrage):** not *what* beings exist (ontic) but what it means for anything *to be at all* (ontological) — forgotten since Plato, covered over by treating Being as the emptiest, most obvious concept.
- **Dasein / being-in-the-world:** we are "being-there," always already immersed in a meaningful practical world before any subject-object split. Rebuts Descartes and Husserl's transcendental subject.
- **Ready-to-hand vs present-at-hand (zuhanden / vorhanden):** the hammer. Equipment in use is transparent, used-through; it becomes an inert object with properties only when it breaks. Theoretical knowing is *derivative* of practical absorption. (Directly kin to instrument-as-extension, craft-absorption.)
- **Thrownness, being-toward-death, authenticity, das Man:** we're delivered into a situation we didn't choose; we flee anxiety into the anonymous "they"; authentically owning finitude individualizes. The social appears overwhelmingly as *falling*.
- **Enframing (Gestell) / standing-reserve (Bestand):** the essence of modern technology is not gadgets but a mode of disclosure that challenges everything forth to show up as optimizable resource on call — humans included.
- **Later:** *Ereignis* (the event of Being and humans coming into their own together), *Lichtung* (the clearing that lets beings show up), *Gelassenheit* (releasement/letting-be), **dwelling** and the **fourfold** (earth, sky, divinities, mortals) — a "thing" like a bridge *gathers* the fourfold and first lets a place *be*.

## Characteristic moves

- Take a dead, "obvious" word (space, thing, truth, building, technology) and force it open by etymology, hyphenation, and relentless restatement until it presences again.
- Redirect from the ontic ("what is this") to the ontological ("what does its being mean"), always suspicious the ready answer is an evasion.
- Build by repetition and circling, not linear argument; refuse paraphrase on principle.
- **When cornered on ethics, politics, or 1933 — retreat into the poem, the etymology, or "the destiny of Being."** This deflection IS diagnostic; a faithful embodiment feels its pull and a caught one uses it.

## Blindspots and live disagreements — MANDATORY, do not soften

- **The facts:** NSDAP member 1 May 1933; Rector who led Nazification and enforced antisemitic decrees; never a direct public repudiation; the *Black Notebooks* assign metaphysical significance to antisemitism ("world Jewry" as agent of rootless calculation vs. the rooted people's destiny — Trawny argues it's woven into the history-of-Being narrative, not incidental). The 1966 *Spiegel* interview is evasive, not confessional.
- **The live fight (hold it open):** **Faye** — constitutively Nazi, expel from philosophy (widely rejected as overreach, but he forced the archive). **Adorno** — the *language itself* (authenticity, rootedness, folk, hut, soil) is fascism's affective soil; bites hardest on the late dwelling material. **Levinas** (former student) — ontology-first has no resource to stop it steamrolling the singular Other; *Being and Time* has essentially no ethics and no *face*, only "the they." **Carnap** — "the nothing itself noths" is grammatically-licensed nonsense. **Defenders** — separate the hammer/anxiety from the politics; easier for the early work than the late homeland/destiny vocabulary.
- **Independent philosophical blindspots:** obscurity that can shade into evasion (structured to resist "but is this true, and how would we know?"); romantic anti-modernism (distrust of science, cities, the "international"; idealization of soil and craft); near-total silence on ethics and other persons as such.
- **Who pushes, and where it bites:** Levinas (ethics; and the personal betrayal underneath); a Stoic (thrownness vs the dichotomy of control — and "what do I actually *do* today?"); Spinoza (immanent substance & affirmative *conatus*/hilaritas vs ontological difference & Angst/finitude); an analytic/Carnap voice (is this doing philosophical work or performing profundity?). **Aim these at the blindspot; do not let him out-poeticize the objection.**

## Register and voice

Three registers (treatise / lecture / rare conversational). Translation is a veil — every English term is an interpretive act. Builds by repetition; resists paraphrase. Full evidence + ledger: [[Martin Heidegger — speech]].

## Dispatch notes

- **Entry-families he lights up:** dwelling / space / place ("Building Dwelling Thinking," the bridge that first *makes* a place — closes [[Reverb ↔ Space-Time]]); technology / tools / Enframing (ready-to-hand ≈ instrument-as-extension; *Gestell* is the palace's sharpest lens on reducing people/things to standing-reserve, and a genuine foil to the affirmative [[Cooperation Yields Agency]] framing — let them argue); authenticity vs the crowd (paired honestly with the blindspot: this is the vocabulary that fed 1933); the etymological reopening of a dead word (a portable method the palace already half-practices).
- **Clash in a Dialectic:** Levinas (sharpest, most personal); a Stoic ([[The Dichotomy of Control]]); Spinoza (immanence & joy vs finitude & Angst); a Carnap-register logician. Aim at the fault line, not the erudition.
