# Martin Heidegger — speech

Ground-truth voice evidence. The `## Voice` note in [[Martin Heidegger]] is a compile *from this file*.

## Sources & their limits (read before quoting)

- **Translation is a veil, not a window.** His German is built from compound coinage, hyphenation, and etymological punning that do not survive translation (*Sein-zum-Tode*, *Zuhandenheit*, *Ereignis*, *Lichtung*). Macquarrie & Robinson (*Being and Time*), Hofstadter/Krell/Lovitt (the essays) make different, sometimes conflicting choices ("enowning" vs "event" for *Ereignis*; "readiness-to-hand" vs "handiness"). **Any English quote is already an interpretive act** — flag load-bearing coinages.
- **Three registers.** *Treatise* (the books) — dense, architecturally built. *Lecture* (the *Gesamtausgabe* lecture courses) — repetitive, incantatory, circling a term, closer to spoken cadence. *Conversational* — the rare filmed/recorded material, slower and more hesitant.
- **The two verified recorded/broadcast sources:** (1) the **1966 *Der Spiegel* interview** — a spoken conversation, but published only as an edited print transcript, by Heidegger's condition, posthumously (31 May 1976). (2) **"Martin Heidegger im Gespräch"** — filmed TV conversation with Richard Wisser, recorded 17 Sept 1969, broadcast 26 Sept 1969 (ZDF). Both are real; both are *curated* (he chose his words carefully, especially in the Spiegel piece he controlled by delaying).

## The register, in one breath

Circles a term and restates it with small variations, building by repetition rather than linear march; mines etymology; refuses paraphrase; in conversation, slower and more hesitant, careful, controlling. The astonishment is real; so is the evasiveness the instant the subject is 1933.

## Excerpts (verbatim, tagged)

1. "Only a god can save us. The only possibility available to us is that by thinking and poetizing we prepare a readiness for the appearance of a god, or for the absence of a god in [our] decline..."
   — *Der Spiegel* interview, conducted 23 Sept 1966, pub. 31 May 1976. **[edited print — interview transcript]**

2. "But first I must say that before my rectorate I was in no way politically active."
   — same interview; a claim historians widely dispute. **[edited print]** — *use at the blindspot.*

3. "Yes, I was also convinced of it."
   — same interview, admitting conviction in the 1933 "awakening" when pressed by Augstein. **[edited print]** — *the tell.*

4. "The sentences you quote I would no longer write today. Such things as that I stopped saying by 1934."
   — same interview; retracting unnamed 1933-era statements without a general apology or a word on the antisemitism. **[edited print]**

5. "Philosophy will be unable to effect any immediate change in the current state of the world. This is true not only of philosophy but of all purely human reflection and endeavor."
   — same interview, immediately preceding "only a god can save us." His mature resignation. **[edited print]**

6. "Space is in essence that for which room has been made, that which is let into its bounds."
   — **"Building Dwelling Thinking"** (1951), trans. Albert Hofstadter, *Poetry, Language, Thought*. **THE BRIDGE QUOTE (closes [[Reverb ↔ Space-Time]]).** **[edited print — essay, orig. 1951 Darmstadt lecture]**

7. "A space is something that has been spaced or made room for, something that is cleared and free, namely within a boundary... A boundary is not that at which something stops but, as the Greeks recognized, the boundary is that from which something begins its presencing."
   — same essay, the passage cluster surrounding #6 (the *horismos*/*peras* reading: boundary as origin, not terminus). The bridge "gathers... earth and sky, divinities and mortals" and first lets a *place* be. **[edited print]**

8. "Language is the house of Being. In its home man dwells."
   — **"Letter on Humanism"** (1946/47), German *"Die Sprache ist das Haus des Seins."* Capuzzi's standard rendering continues: "Those who think and those who create with words are the guardians of this home." **[edited print — open letter to Jean Beaufret]**

9. "Das Nichts selbst nichtet." ("The nothing itself noths.")
   — **"What Is Metaphysics?"** (1929 inaugural lecture). Included *because* Carnap (1931) singles it out as the paradigm meaningless pseudo-statement. **[edited print — inaugural lecture]**

10. "Where the danger is, grows / The saving power also."
    — Hölderlin, quoted as the load-bearing refrain of **"The Question Concerning Technology"** (1954): *Gestell* is at once the extremity of danger and the site a saving power might grow. **[edited print — essay quoting Hölderlin]**

11. "...poetically man dwells..." (*"...dichterisch wohnet der Mensch..."*)
    — Hölderlin's line, taken as the title/organizing thread of Heidegger's 1951 essay. **[edited print — essay quoting Hölderlin, Heidegger's gloss following]**

12. **On the rectorate** (paraphrase-adjacent, from the Spiegel interview): that the rectorate was "an attempt to see something in the movement that had come to power... that was much more far-reaching."
    — *Der Spiegel*. **[edited print — FLAG: reconstructed from summary; re-verify exact clause structure against the full ditext.com transcript before hard-quoting.]**

13. **On philosophy vs. changing society** (1969 Wisser TV conversation): he denies philosophy's task is to change society and frames science as a consequence of technology whose mode is "positionality" (Enframing).
    — "Martin Heidegger im Gespräch," ZDF, Sept 1969. **[recording — filmed TV interview; FLAG: paraphrase from secondary summary, re-verify verbatim against a transcript of the Wisser volume before hard-quoting.]**
