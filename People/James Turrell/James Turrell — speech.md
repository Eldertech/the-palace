## The register, in one breath

Measured, contemplative, technically precise about apparatus, then reaching for scale and myth (Plato, the Quaker light, the celestial) without warning — and stating the mysticism *flat*, as fact, not performance. Clipped and a little cold when pressed on the viewer's convenience.

## Excerpts (tagged; confidence noted)

### Recording — the Quaker root and perception (Whittaker, primary)

1. "My grandmother used to tell me that as you sat in Quaker silence you were to go inside to greet the light. That expression stuck with me."
   — the seed of the whole practice; later the literal name of his Chestnut Hill Skyspace. **[recording · confirmed, primary]**

2. "There is a truth in light. That is, you only get light by burning material."
   — mysticism stated as physics. **[recording · confirmed, primary]**

3. "I make spaces that apprehend light for our perception, and in some way gather it, or seem to hold it."
   — his own compression of what the work does. **[recording · confirmed, primary]**

4. "We generate light at night in the cities to offset our fear of each other, but lighting the night sky cuts off access to the universe."
   — **[recording · confirmed, primary]**

### Edited print — perception is the medium (Smithsonian 2003)

5. "I like to use light as a material, but my medium is really perception."
   — the thesis in one line. **[edited print · confirmed]**

6. "I want you to sense yourself sensing. To see yourself seeing. To be aware of how you are forming the reality you see."
   — the root of "seeing yourself see." **[edited print · confirmed]**

7. "We're not very aware of how we create reality. My work is just a gentle reminder that we're making this world, that we shape it, literally, we color it, literally."
   — **[edited print · confirmed]**

8. "It's a beautiful geologic structure, and I want it to look as untouched as possible when I'm done."
   — of Roden Crater, from which 1.3M+ cubic yards have been excavated. **[edited print · confirmed]** — *the blindspot in one line: total intervention described as leaving-untouched.*

### Recording — the tell (Whittaker, primary)

9. "That's fine. You can let that go. Because they can take care of that, and then I don't need to be bothered with that audience that's impatient."
   — asked about his work demanding patience from viewers. **[recording · confirmed, primary]** — *not an artist who has abdicated control to the viewer; one who declines responsibility for the control he keeps.*

10. "It's not my business, or even my intent to in any way, affirm your taste... If anything, it's to challenge that, and expand it."
    — more authorial than "I just make conditions" admits. **[recording · confirmed, primary]**

### Flagged — high-confidence-in-substance, not primary-verified

11. "You are looking at you looking."
    — his signature compression of "no object, no image, no focus"; sourced to a Guardian piece not directly fetched. Consistent with his verified style. **[secondhand · very likely accurate, verify against primary before hard-citing]**

12. "To some degree, to control light, I have to have a way to form it, so I use form almost like the stretcher bar of a canvas."
    — useful for the blindspot (form as a modest framing device) but aggregator-sourced only. **[secondhand · verify before hard-citing]**
