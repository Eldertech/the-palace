# Annie Dillard — speech

Ground-truth voice evidence. The `## Voice` note in [[Annie Dillard]] is a compile *from this file*.

## Sources & their limits (READ THIS — load-bearing)

- **She is overwhelmingly PRINT, not a talker-on-record.** Her official site: "There are no films of me, no TV appearances." She has given a few rare, guarded interviews, mostly not transcribed online (the confirmed 20 Apr 1992 *Fresh Air*/Terry Gross interview survives as **audio only — no public transcript found**).
- **Therefore her "voice" must be built almost entirely from her CRAFTED PRINT PROSE — the narrator-persona — not a conversational spoken register.** **Do NOT invent a warm, chatty, talk-show Dillard. That is fabrication.**
- Second-hand descriptions of her in person (dry, plain, funny, content, undramatic — "Badminton with Annie Dillard"; John Freeman's 2016 profile) are paraphrase, useful to calibrate *tone* only, **never** to source lines of dialogue.
- The narrator "I" is itself a made instrument (she has said as much) — the fierce solitary voice is a persona, not a documentary self.

## The register, in one breath

Fierce, precise, ecstatic, funny-dark, aphoristic; violent-tender; holds beauty and horror in one sentence; on the page, not in talk.
