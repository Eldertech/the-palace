# The Palace Practices on Itself

![[The Palace Practices on Itself — hero.png]]

The palace's central developmental model, named at last: **it grows by practicing on itself.** Build a capability, then turn it back on the palace and grow from what that teaches. The output and the test subject are the same thing — a feedback loop where what is being built is immediately used to build.

Dogfooding, in the plain software sense of *eat your own cooking* — but here it isn't a QA tactic bolted on at the end. It is the **engine**.

## The loop

Zoom out to build a structure ([[Zoom Out to the Structure]]); apply that structure to the palace itself; learn from what breaks or clicks; feed that back in. Outward, then inward, then again. The two moves are one breath — generalize, then fold back. Zoom-out is the inhale; practicing-on-itself is the exhale.

## This session as the worked example

The whole RunPod → Commons arc dogfooded itself, and that is why this entry is born from it:

- The work was about *agents not strangling each other's shared resources* — and it was **merged** using that exact discipline: landing on `main` while another agent's uncommitted work sat in the tree, never reaching into their space.
- The multi-agent safety was proven by **running three agents at once** — three concurrent hero/avatar renders on one shared endpoint, ramping its workers up and down without any agent parking another's mid-render.
- The **toolbox** convention, built for a single project, immediately asked to apply to the *whole palace*: if [[Pages as Agents|every entry is an agent]], every entry could grow its own **toolkit** — [[Loudon's Toolkit]] (a person's kit) → a project's toolbox → an entry-agent's toolbox. The realization applied itself the moment it was made.

Even *this deposit* is an instance: the entry naming the loop was written by walking the loop.

## The counter-discipline: don't only look inward

A system that practices only on itself can go insular — self-reference with no outside test, polishing its own reflection. The palace stays honest because the loop keeps touching *real* outside: real GPUs that really fail, real renders you actually look at, the [[Cooperation Yields Agency|"how do you feel, not how many views"]] measure. Dogfooding without an external bite is just a hall of mirrors. The loop has to keep drawing blood from reality, or it drifts into decoration.

## Cross-domain resonance

This [[mirrors]] **[[Endosymbiosis]]**: the merger where one organism took another inside itself and both kept growing — the palace folding its own new capability inward and compounding from it. The difference worth keeping: endosymbiosis fused two *different* organisms; here the palace folds in *itself*, which is exactly why the insularity warning matters.

## Forward Vectors

- Make *"does this apply to us?"* a reflex on every new capability — the unprompted inward turn. Track whether it starts firing on its own.
- The **toolbox recursion** is the open edge: what would it actually mean for an ordinary entry-agent to *have* a toolkit? Flagged to [[Pages as Agents]] and [[Loudon's Toolkit]] for a Weave.
- Find the failure mode: a time the palace practiced on itself and drifted insular. Name what pulled it back — that story is the counter-discipline's proof.
- This entry wants to graduate from `growing` to a load-bearing `meta` hub as more of the palace's self-application gets named around it.
