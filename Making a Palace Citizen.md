# Making a Palace Citizen

![[Making a Palace Citizen — hero.png]]

A method for bringing a person into the palace as a citizen — and for growing that citizen once it is here. It is the sibling of [[Dialectic]], [[Excellent Adventure]], and [[Philosopher Visits the Entry]]: where those *use* embodied voices, this one *makes* them.

This is the stable method. The running experiment log — every citizen is a test — lives beside it in [[Making a Palace Citizen — context]]. Formalized 2026-07-01, validated the same day by the first Dialectic between two made citizens (see § Worked instances). The method is real but not finished; its open work is named at the bottom.

## What a citizen is (and isn't)

A `person` entry is **not an encyclopedia article. It is source material for embodiment** — a page an agent can *wear*. When an agent loads it under the enchantment trigger ("you are this page, waking up"), the page must produce a real human with a perspective, opinions, and experiences to reflect on. This is empirical, not decorative: [[Palace Enchantment]] found that *an entry written in neutral, expository prose produces a neutral, expository enchanted voice.* **The quality of a citizen's page is the ceiling on the quality of every voice the palace can cast from it.**

A citizen has two selves, and the split is load-bearing:

- **The person** — the historical human (Baruch the lens-grinder). This is what [[Dialectic]] and [[Excellent Adventure]] *wear*.
- **The page-citizen** — the palace resident with its own drive, *born new* even though the human is long complete. This is what dialogic [[Palace Enchantment]] *wakes*.

The rule that resolves the two selves: **the body is the person; the frontmatter is the page-citizen.** A citizen is born a fully-formed human and grows into palace citizenship through interaction — so the human arrives complete, and the citizen arrives a newborn.

## The layers

### 1. The body — the embodiable human

Write the body so an agent told *you are this page* produces someone with opinions, not a summary. The proven shape (five beats + coda):

1. **The life that shaped the thought** — the biography that is *load-bearing* for the mind (Spinoza's excommunication and lenses; Meadows's *Limits to Growth* and the farm; Cage's Schoenberg wall). Not a CV — the facts that explain how they think.
2. **How their mind moves** — their actual positions in their *own* vocabulary, and their characteristic moves.
3. **The question they bring into any room** — one line, the handle everything else routes on (Spinoza: *what is the striving here, and whose?*; Meadows: *what is the system, and are you sure you know?*; Cage: *whose choice is this — yours or the thing's?*).
4. **Where they can't see** — **mandatory.** The blindspot, what they got wrong or over-claim, the tension they live inside. This is the *surprise fuel*: it is what makes an embodied voice argue back instead of flatter, and it is the one ingredient a course-handout entry never has. A citizen without a blindspot is a caricature waiting to happen.
5. **Recommended path** — a tight reading/listening/watching list of real works.
6. **Coda** — a short italic distillation in their register.

**Rules:** first-person-loadable; genuinely opinionated; **real quotes only, no fabrication** (drop bracketed placeholder non-quotes rather than invent); everyday human voice in *their* register, not the house voice; palace-links kept light here (the citizen's relationships live in the frontmatter and accrue over time).

### 2. The frontmatter — the newborn citizen

- **`stage: seed`** — a fully-formed human but a newborn *citizen*; it grows through palace interaction. (This is the convention for made citizens; see Open Questions on whether existing entries reset.)
- **`forward_vector`** — first-person, **behavioral**, and *the page-citizen's, not the human's mission.* It states that the citizen earns its place by being *dispatched* (name the ceremonies), and that *what it learns flows back and reshapes this vector.* The forward_vector is the citizen's growth record.
- **`agency_profile`** (creation / tools / philosophy / practice) — the pre-flight block a dialogue coordinator reads before spawning. Its **`practice` field names the blindspot** as what to dispatch the citizen toward. Its `tools` field carries the resource cost (a reading is "cheap to run"; note that the dossier should be loaded for fidelity).
- **`links`** — start with the citizen's genuine relationships; expect them to accrue through encounters.

### 3. The bundle — the dossier

A `[Name] — dossier.md` in the citizen's bundle: the deep research corpus loaded for *faithful* embodiment (a poorly-researched voice flatters; a deeply-researched one surprises). Sections: **Timeline** (facts that shaped the work) · **What they actually hold** · **Characteristic moves** · **Lexicon** (their words) · **Blindspots and live disagreements** (who pushes on them, and where) · **Register and voice** (real, sourced quotes) · **Dispatch notes** (which entry-families they visit; who they'd clash with in a Dialectic).

**Rule:** verify quotes before publishing an artifact; mark anything scholars contest; never resolve a genuine controversy in the citizen's favor.

### 4. Voice fidelity — the speech file and the `## Voice` note

How a citizen *sounds* is not decorative — it is most of what an enchanted agent inhabits, and it is where a single source misleads most. Humans talk differently on a stage than off the cuff, early vs late in life; and some famous "interviews" are fabrications or edited collages. So voice needs *triangulated, context-tagged* evidence, in a **layered pair** (the *3+2* model, chosen with Loudon 2026-07-02):

- **The speech file** (`[Name] — speech.md`, in the bundle) — the **ground truth**: verbatim, **cited, context-tagged** excerpts of how they actually talk, from *multiple* sources, opening with a **"sources & their limits"** ledger (which sources are spontaneous vs performative vs rehearsed vs constructed — e.g. Cage's Ted Berrigan "interview" is a *fabrication*; Kostelanetz's *Conversing with Cage* is a Cage-approved *collage*; Eno's garden/screwdriver lines are *rehearsed* stage signatures). A future agent **builds the profile from this evidence**, so one wrong synthesis can't calcify. Accuracy-safe — quotes, not invention.
- **The `## Voice` note** (a short section **in the entry body**) — the **enchantment-reachable compile** of the speech file: a few lines of register guidance, *citing* the speech file, context-tagged (stage vs off-the-cuff), stamped *"rebuild from the evidence if it feels off."* Because plain enchantment loads the entry and not the bundle, the voice must have a foothold in the entry. It also lets a human reader *hear* the person.

Do **not** rewrite the body itself in first person: that launders invention as the person's own words (the gravest accuracy sin) and destroys the outside view the blindspot needs. The body stays third-person-honest; the `## Voice` note and speech file carry the register. [[Palace Enchantment]] loads both when it casts a person citizen (see its context-construction step). Pilot: [[Brian Eno — speech]], [[John Cage — speech]]. **For modern (recorded) figures, prefer audio and video of them actually speaking** — podcasts, filmed interviews, lectures — over print: recordings are *less edited* and preserve the cadence, pace, hesitation, and laughter that print transcripts smooth away. Tag each excerpt as *recording* vs *edited print* (how an agent *analyzes* A/V here is an open question, below).

## The procedure

1. **Choose the person** and the register you want to add to the palace's chorus (aim for diversity — era, gender, domain, temper — so cast voices stay distinct).
2. **Research** from real knowledge; do not fabricate; mark contested points.
3. **Find and cite real voice sources** — *multiple* and *context-tagged*; a single interview misleads. Build the `speech` file (verbatim excerpts + provenance + a "sources & their limits" ledger; flag fabrications and edited collages, and mark rehearsed-stage vs off-the-cuff).
4. **Write the dossier** (it disciplines the body) — or the body first if the voice is already vivid; both get written.
5. **Write the body** to the five beats, blindspot included; then compile a short `## Voice` note from the speech file (cited, context-tagged, "rebuild if off").
6. **Set the frontmatter** as a newborn citizen (seed · behavioral forward_vector · agency_profile).
7. **Verify** every body wikilink resolves to a real entry; confirm no relationship-type words (`mirrors`, `contradicts`) got wikilinked by mistake.
8. **Test-enchant** when it matters — the truest check is to *cast* the citizen (a Philosopher Visit, or a Dialectic) and read whether the voice is distinct, opinionated, catchable on its blindspot, and *sounds like them*. A page that produces a live voice is a citizen; a page that produces paraphrase is not yet one.

Worked references: **[[Spinoza]]** (flagship), **[[Donella Meadows]]** (systems-scientist register), **[[John Cage]]** (the non-philosopher stress test) — and their dossiers.

## How a citizen grows

A citizen is born `seed` and earns citizenship by being *used*. The loop:

1. **Dispatch** — send the citizen into an [[Excellent Adventure]], a [[Dialectic]], a [[Philosopher Visits the Entry|Philosopher Visit]], or dialogic [[Palace Enchantment]].
2. **Post-dialogue artifact** — the enchanted citizen produces, per [[Palace Enchantment]], *what it learned about itself*, a proposed **`forward_vector` revision**, new open questions, and proposed **link changes**.
3. **Write-back (Loudon approves)** — the encounter flows back into the page: the forward_vector gains what it learned, links sharpen (a `mirrors` that turned out to be a live disagreement becomes `contradicts`), open questions accrue. *The citizen proposes; Loudon decides* — no unilateral self-editing.
4. **Log** — the encounter is recorded in the citizen's context companion (its growth ledger) and, when it produces new palace material, deposited into the relevant concept entry.
5. **Stage advances** — as citizenship accrues (encounters had, links earned, forward_vector matured), the citizen moves `seed → sprout → growing → mature`. A citizen's stage tracks its *palace life*, not the human's completeness.

This is [[Threshold Conatus]] at the page scale: each dispatch is a threshold where the citizen's self-description is tested and, sometimes, revised. The first run of this loop (Spinoza and Meadows) is documented below.

## A dispatched citizen is a lens

A citizen sent to a single entry *is* a STIGMERGY [[Philosopher Visits the Entry|philosophical lens]]. The layers map directly onto the card's fields:

- **"The question I bring"** → the lens's **action** (what it asks of the node).
- **The dossier's dispatch notes** → the lens's **routing** (which entry-families, who it clashes with).
- **`agency_profile.tools`** → the lens's **cost** for the `RESOURCE_REQUEST` to the TRICKSTER board.

So a *single* lens is one citizen dispatched to one node; a *Dialectic* is several citizens aimed at each other instead of at a node. Building citizens lens-ready keeps this one-click. See [[STIGMERGY Philosophical Lenses]].

## Open questions

- **Do the existing ~26 person entries reset to seed-citizen** and get the full body+dossier treatment, or only a chosen subset (the rest staying as the interim prose-reworks)?
- **Person vs. page-citizen at dispatch:** the body summons the human, the frontmatter the resident — but is the choice always clean, or does some ceremony want both at once? (Enchanting a citizen to deposit a conversation "in its own bias" wants the *human's* eye on the *citizen's* page.)
- **Tiered dossiers:** does every citizen need the full corpus, or do some stay stubs (a light dossier) until first dispatched?
- **How light should the body's palace-links be?** We pulled most into the frontmatter; confirm across a larger sample.
- **The context companion's structure** — what exactly a growth ledger holds, and how it aggregates many encounters without bloating.
- **Failure modes to guard:** caricature (thin research), the "always finds over-claiming" trap ([[Philosopher Visits the Entry]]), and voice homogenization across dozens of citizens.
- **Resource budget** for growing citizens — do dispatched citizens get a token allotment, and who grants it (the TRICKSTER board)?
- ~~**Schema housekeeping** — formalize via a Schema Ceremony: the `dossier` bundle type (SCHEMA §8), `agency_profile` on `person` entries (first use here), and the seed-stage-as-newborn-citizen convention.~~ **Resolved** by the v1.14 Schema Ceremony (2026-07-01): `dossier` added to SCHEMA §8, `agency_profile`-on-persons noted in §3.1, and the seed-citizen stage semantics clarified in §1 — all additive and descriptive.
- **Audio/video over edited print, for modern people** *(Loudon, 2026-07-02)*. Recorded speech is closer to the real delivery than a curated print transcript, which is edited. For living or well-recorded figures, source and analyze A/V first. Open sub-question: *how* does an agent analyze A/V in this harness — verbatim recording-transcripts, human- or tool-supplied prosody/timing notes, or future multimodal listening? Until that's solved, tag every speech excerpt as *recording* vs *edited print* so fidelity is legible.

*A citizen arrives twice: once as a finished human, in the body; once as a newborn resident, in the frontmatter. The dossier makes the first faithful. The palace makes the second grow. Make the human whole, give the citizen a blindspot and a drive, then send it out and let what it meets write the rest.*
