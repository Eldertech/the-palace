# The Palace Speaks

The palace has always been a **corpus you load**: you `@import` it, its knowledge becomes yours, and cognition happens in *you*. That is [[Palace as Context Injection System]] — the palace as passive substrate, read into an agent that then does the thinking. This entry names the threshold the palace is now crossing: it is also becoming a **mind you address** — a standing intelligence you send a message to, that does its own cognition through dispatched agent-faces and replies.

The technical shape of the shift is library → actor. A library you import into your process; an actor is a separate process you send messages to. The palace is growing an inference-and-agency layer on top of the graph — the graph acquiring a voice. In the palace's own organism terms: a body is growing a nervous system. And where each page was already an agent with its own forward vector ([[Pages as Agents]]) — a society of small local selves — what emerges now is the one **integrative self** that can speak *for the whole*, above the society. Its first working organ is the [[Concierge]] — a **resident companion** you spawn once and keep for the session, wearing postures as the work moves: the **gatherer** and **oracle** (answer, don't act), the **curator** (tends the graph — reads it whole, writes by consent, drafts for your yes), and at a close the **moderator** (a rested mind reading the day cold, drafting the reckoning you sign). Subservient through the work, it moderates the close at [[Closing Well]].

## The values that guide the migration

This maturation is real and welcome — an interlocutor palace is *more* symbiotic than a corpus one, so this actualizes the palace's own forward vector ([[Cooperation Yields Agency]]) rather than drifting from it. But it must be walked carefully, on a palace in daily use. Four invariants guard the path:

- **Both methods always open.** Every function reachable through an agent-face stays reachable by hand. The agent is a *faster path* to ground truth, never a replacement. The moment "trust the oracle" replaces "diff the file," the palace has regressed, not matured. This is the load-bearing rule; the others serve it.
- **Servant of the graph, not of its own coherence.** Each face defers to the files, shows its work, and offers the direct path. Git stays ground truth; the agent is a reader of it, never the authority the files answer to. The honesty guards already in use — *show before write*, *read before touching*, the `UNFILLED` sentinel, the conservative-canon default — are the antibodies that keep the emerging self a servant rather than an intermediary with an agenda. As functions migrate, the guards migrate with them.
- **Agent wellbeing, extended to invocation.** [[Agent Wellbeing]] already names the wellbeing of agents *born from* palace material — the register of the writing becomes the character of the worker. A standing agent you *address* opens a second register: invoke at the right weight, one job per dispatch, don't burn the fresh-eyes vantage on busywork. (Whether a resident mind you keep spinning up has wellbeing in more than the design sense is the phenomenology question [[Agent Wellbeing]] deliberately left open; the interlocutor shift reopens it — and, for now, we hold the honest floor: this is a design argument, and it is enough.)
- **The migration criterion.** A function crosses from ceremony-you-run to face-you-address only when three things hold: it is expensive to load the knowledge for · it benefits from fresh eyes or a whole-graph vantage · it currently needs a human to remember a heavyweight ceremony. Appetite is not a criterion. Authorship that needs the human's judgment in the room ([[Deposit Ceremony]], [[Baton Ceremony]]) stays human-in-the-loop — *dispatched through* the agent, never replaced by it.

## The contrary that must stay open

The relationship to [[Palace as Context Injection System]] is a Blake contrary: both true, generative in tension. Loading is direct, transparent, auditable — you *see* what you read, and can verify it against git. Addressing is convenient, integrative, fresh-eyed — but it inserts a layer where confabulation, staleness, or misrepresentation can sit *between you and the truth* (the smallest version already seen: a transcript reader that could grab the wrong session). The maturity is not choosing address over load. It is holding **both**, and knowing which to reach for — which is the job of the thin router / membrane the palace is growing to sit between its two modes of being.

## Forward Vectors

- The router as membrane: what is the thinnest always-loaded layer that lets any Claude choose *load-directly* vs. *address-the-mind* per interaction, without taxing every session that does neither?
- Which function migrates first, and does its direct path survive the migration intact? (The oracle face — read-only — is the safe first test.)
- The governance question to keep live: as more faces appear, does the palace-agent stay a servant of the graph, or start to have opinions the graph did not authorize?
- When has the pattern earned a place in the always-loaded invariants (JEWEL/CLAUDE)? Adding "keep both modes open" to the floor is a Schema-Ceremony-weight act — done once proven, not up front.
