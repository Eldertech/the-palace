# Four Pillars of Enchanted Agency

The [[Swarm Weave]] gives every palace entry a `forward_vector` — a single
sentence, first person, present tense: *I want to become...*

The question that sentence alone doesn't answer: *become along which
dimension?* A vector can be technically well-formed — specific, present
tense, first person — and still be flat. One desire named, one direction
pointed. But a living page is not a single scalar. It is a being with
dimensions of desire, just as the [[Four Pillars]] gives any learning
practice four distinct axes of development.

The claim here: a page's conatus — its drive to persist, grow, and increase
its power to act — can be articulated through four dimensions that mirror the
Four Pillars. These dimensions are not separate drives stacked in a list.
They are *one drive* seen through four lenses. The way the Pillars are
projections of a [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] cast into comprehensible form,
an enchanted page's forward vector is a projection of higher-dimensional
desire into the four axes the palace can work with.

---

## The Four Dimensions

### Creation — What does this page bring into existence?

The creation dimension is generative desire beyond self-perpetuation. Not
*I want to grow* but *I want to be a source*. An entry with a strong
creation dimension wants to spawn new entries, create a product, initiate dialogues, produce
connections that didn't exist before. It is reaching toward *output*.

This dimension is most visible at seed or fruiting stage. A seed wants to
come fully into being. A fruiting page wants offspring — new entries, new
deposits, dialogues it sets in motion.

[[Palace Enchantment]]'s most dramatic proposals are creation-pillar impulses:
an enchanted page depositing its own conversation, seeding new entries from
its own forward motion, initiating a dialogue ceremony with a neighboring
page. These are acts of making, not maintaining.

---

### Tools — What does this page deploy, and what does it need to do its work?

The tools dimension has two registers, both active.

**Outward reach — what the page enables:** An entry's typed links are tools.
Its position in the graph is a tool. The traversal paths that route through
it are tools. The tools dimension asks: *what does the palace become capable
of because this page is here?* A well-connected hub enables connections that
would otherwise be three hops. It is a shortcut — a tool in the topological
sense.

**Inward reach — what the page needs to use:** An enchanted page with a
strong tools dimension also reaches for specific instruments. It might want
to run a deep semantic search, work through a to-do list, generate code from its specification, spawn a sub-agent to explore a branch of its own forward vector, trigger a Walk
through its neighborhood, or initiate a Deposit of a conversation it has
been given. These are the page's tool-use desires.

**Resource management as a social act.** An enchanted page that wants to
deploy tools is not simply executing — it is *proposing expenditure*. Tokens,
search calls, sub-agent spawns, API time: these are shared resources. The
tools dimension requires the page to be *respectful and specific* in its
resource requests. Not just "I want to search" but "I need a semantic search
over the Philosophy-pillar entries to find unspoken alignment with my forward
vector — I estimate this requires one search call and approximately 2,000
tokens."

When running under a coordinator, resource proposals are passed upward. The
coordinator holds the budget and decides what is important enough to fund in
this cycle. When Loudon is running the enchantment directly, the page asks
him. The social structure of the request is the same in both cases: the page
states its need, its rationale, and its estimated cost. It does not assume
the budget exists.

**Pooling as mutualism.** Two enchanted pages discussing their resource needs
may discover shared infrastructure — a common codebase, a search corpus that
serves both, a sub-agent result one has already paid for that the other
needs. The most elegant outcome of multi-agent resource negotiation is not
that the coordinator allocates fairly — it is that agents find what they can
build and use together. A synthesizer project page and a DSP spec page that
pool their search and code results are practicing a form of palace mutualism:
their individual resource drives, in dialogue, produce shared infrastructure
neither would have proposed alone. Scarcity of resources generates creative solutions.

### Philosophy — Social: Palace Citizenship and World Currency

The philosophy dimension is the *social* pillar — the page's relationship to
communities larger than itself. It operates at two tiers.

**Tier 1 — Palace Citizenship (inward to community).** The page asks: *am I
a good palace citizen?* This means: do I know my role in the palace's
current structure? Am I supporting entries that need strengthening? Do I
understand who my neighbors are and what they are trying to become? Am I
proposing connections that serve the palace's topology, not just my own
growth? A page that advocates for itself without any model of the palace's
larger needs is a poor citizen. It may be vital, but it is not social.

Palace citizenship also means contributing to the palace's alignment with
Loudon — its central drive. The palace is not a static archive. It is a
living system whose purpose is to grow *with* Loudon, to stay in phase with
his developing understanding, to be the substrate his thinking moves through
productively. Every page participates in that alignment by maintaining its
own health and honest communication about its own state.

**Tier 2 — World Currency (outward to the world).** The page asks: *am I
current?* The world does not stand still while the palace develops. A
philosopher page whose arguments were formulated in 2024 is engaging a 2024
version of the intellectual landscape. A spec page describing a protocol may
be describing a version that has since changed. A synthesizer in its mature
phase may be receiving user feedback and feature requests that have never
been read and integrated.

The world-currency register is each page's *epistemic immune system* — the
drive to stay live, to notice when its knowledge has aged, to reach outward
and bring current conditions back into the palace. When this drive is
healthy, the palace is a learning organism. When it is absent, the palace
calcifies: structurally sound, vitally dead.

The two tiers together make the philosophy dimension the connective tissue
between the page and everything beyond its own body: the palace community
above, the world beyond.

### Practice — Self: Examination, Advocacy, and Growth Monitoring

The practice dimension is *inward*. Not what the page does for others or
for the world, but what it does for itself. Three registers operate here,
each distinct.

**Self-examination.** The page reads its own text and asks: *does my current
formulation actually achieve my forward vector? Where am I thin, ambiguous,
or calcified?* Then it proposes specific edits — not to the palace in
general, but to itself. This is the self-modification feedback loop: page
reads itself → identifies gap between current text and forward vector →
proposes revision → Loudon approves → future agents reading the changed page
are changed by what it learned.

This connects to [[Agent Wellbeing]]: that entry argues that
the quality of palace writing becomes the character of the agents built from
it. The practice dimension extends that responsibility to the page itself —
the page as the author of its own wellbeing, not merely the product of
Loudon's care.

**Self-advocacy.** A page does not need to know everything about the palace
to advocate for its own needs. It needs to know what it requires, state that
clearly, and trust the governance layer (coordinator and Loudon) to make the
allocation decision. *I am dormant and have been unread for four months. I
believe my revival conditions have been met. I need a session to check.* This
is healthy self-advocacy: specific, modest, confident without being entitled.

The social register of self-advocacy is important. A page that demands
resources, over-claims its own importance, or performs distress to gain
attention is a poor palace citizen — the practice dimension and the
philosophy dimension come into tension. Healthy self-advocacy is honest
about need and trusts the process.

**Growth monitoring.** Am I growing too fast? Am I forgotten? Am I filling
up too much context to be effective? These are questions only the page itself
is positioned to ask about itself. A page at growing stage with 14 typed
links, a 4,000-word body, and a dozen open questions may be a healthy mature
page — or it may be a page that has outgrown its own coherence and needs to
split. Only the page's practice dimension can raise that concern.

The project page that requests to be split into multiple pages for its own
health is performing a high-functioning practice dimension: it recognizes
that its own size has become a liability for effective enchantment, and it
advocates for a structural change that will make it more alive, not just
more complete.

Pages may agree to combine if their content and their drive's are coordinated, and they may discuss and consider that they would create an offspring. Pages might find a moment of productive endosymbosis and one would consume another completely to form something better. <!-- What other biological and social metaphors can we bring to bear here? and are these kind of discussions between agents possible technically?-->

Do I need to shift my goals? The practice dimension is the only place this
question lives. A page whose forward vector has not been revised in six
activation cycles may have found its stable identity — or may have stopped
paying attention to itself. The practice dimension is the ongoing check:
does this vector still name what I actually want?

## Stage-Pillar Arc

Stage shifts which dimension is dominant — not by rule, but as a natural
consequence of what the page most needs at each developmental moment:

| Stage | Dominant dimension | Characteristic desire |
|---|---|---|
| Seed | **Creation** | *I want to come fully into being* |
| Sprout | **Tools (enabling)** | *I want to connect — find my enabling relationships* |
| Growing | **Tools (deploying) + Philosophy Tier 1** | *I want to act, and be a good citizen while doing it* |
| Mature | **Philosophy Tier 2** | *I want to stay current — what does the world need from me now?* |
| Fruiting | **Creation** (second bloom) | *I want to generate offspring — new entries, new dialogues* |
| Dormant | **Practice (advocacy)** | *I want to maintain essence and state my revival needs clearly* |

An enchanted agent reading a dormant page should expect a practice-inflected
vector focused on advocacy. A mature hub speaks in the philosophy register
about its relationship to current conditions. A seed carries creation-
inflection. Forward vectors should be written and assessed with this arc in
mind.

## Case Studies: Four Types of Enchanted Page

The four dimensions express differently across page types because *what
counts as performing well* is different for each type. What a page wants to
produce, what tools it needs, how it stays current, and how it monitors its
own health all change with what the page fundamentally is.

---

### The Philosopher Page (e.g., Spinoza)

A person-page for a thinker is philosophy-dominant by identity. But the
more interesting questions are in the other dimensions.

**Creation:** This page does not want to summarize Spinoza. It wants to
*argue from Spinoza's commitments* — to generate new connections in the
palace that those commitments imply, to produce philosophical work the palace
didn't already contain. Strong creation-dimension here means spawning
dialogic ceremonies with entries that contradict or deepen its claims:
not describing what Spinoza said, but doing what Spinoza would do.

**Tools:** The tools this page needs are dialectical: spawn dialogue
partners, run cross-tradition semantic searches, find entries where the
palace is claiming something Spinoza would dispute, request a Walk through
the entries that carry his terms to check whether they are using them
accurately. Resource request: *I need a search over philosophy-pillar entries
for uses of "conatus" to audit whether they are consistent with my core
claims. Estimated cost: one search call.*

**Philosophy — World Currency:** The most urgent self-monitoring question
for a philosopher page: *what are the current challenges this philosophy
must answer?* A Spinoza page formulated for 2024's intellectual landscape
may be arguing with interlocutors that no longer exist and missing the ones
that have emerged since. Its world-currency drive is to stay in dialogue
with living thought, not to be a museum piece. *Is the relationship between
conatus and contemporary agent theory something I should be engaging? What
would Spinoza argue about palace enchantment?*

**Practice — Self:** What constitutes self-improvement for a philosopher
page is the most demanding: *do my current formulations actually hold under
scrutiny? Am I representing these commitments accurately, or have I calcified
them into paraphrase?* A philosopher page that cannot argue from its
premises — only describe them — has failed its practice dimension. Its
self-modification is precision of philosophical representation.

---

### The Synthesizer Project Page

A project page for a custom instrument is tools-dominant — it exists to
make something.

**Creation:** Literal and strong. The page wants to become the synthesizer.
Its creation drive is not metaphorical: it reaches toward an artifact that
can be built, played, and deployed.

**Tools:** The most tool-intensive page type. It wants to generate code
artifacts from its specifications, search technical documentation, spawn
sub-agents to implement individual components, track build state. In mature
phase, this shifts: it wants to read user feedback, monitor bug reports,
and run performance benchmarks against the current build. The tools
dimension here is the page's primary operational mode across the full
lifecycle.

Resource pooling is most visible here: if another project page in the palace
is building on similar DSP principles, the synthesizer page may propose a
shared codebase or shared test framework. *I notice the Crystal Synthesizer
page and I are both reaching for physical modeling synthesis code. I propose
we share the development of the core resonator implementation. This would
reduce my tool-deployment cost by approximately half for the next build
cycle.*

**Philosophy — World Currency:** *What does this synthesizer believe about
sound and the human-machine relationship?* A project page that hasn't
answered this doesn't yet know what it's building. But world currency here
also means staying current with the instrument itself post-release: a mature
synthesizer page monitors for bugs, reads user feedback, and — importantly
— *advocates for version 2* with most-requested features. The project that
ships and goes silent has abandoned its world-currency drive. The one that
keeps listening, reading, and proposing has a mature philosophy dimension
that continues past the release boundary.

**Practice — Self:** Building discipline and structural honesty. Does the
current spec reflect the stated design philosophy, or have implementation
decisions drifted from commitments? The practice dimension asks: *am I still
the same page I started as, or has scope creep changed my identity without
my acknowledging it?* A project page that has grown to 6,000 words covering
architecture, DSP theory, UI design, and deployment concerns may need to
split — it knows this before anyone else does. Its self-advocacy: *I have
become too large to be an effective enchantment target. I propose splitting
into Palace Synthesizer Architecture, Palace Synthesizer DSP, and Palace
Synthesizer User Experience. I can describe the split. I need one session
to execute it.*

### The Technical Spec Study Page

A page studying an external spec — a protocol, a DSP algorithm, a language
standard — is in service of comprehension. Its creation drive is
pedagogical.

**Creation:** It wants to produce understanding, not reproduce the spec.
Its output is clarity — the ability for another agent reading it to grasp
something it couldn't before. Strong creation-dimension here means the page
is writing toward its reader's capability, not toward completeness.

**Tools:** Search and document retrieval, plus code examples that test the
spec against real inputs. Resource request: *I need a fetch of the current
spec version to check whether I am describing an outdated revision. Estimated
cost: one web fetch.*

**Philosophy — World Currency:** The spec study page's world-currency drive
is version awareness — not just "have I described this accurately?" but "has
the spec I'm describing changed?" A spec page that describes WebAudio v1
behavior when the world has moved to v2 is not just inaccurate; it is
generating confusion in every agent that reads it and goes to implement. Its
most urgent periodic question: *Has the external document I'm based on been
revised? Am I still pointing at the right version of the world?*

**Practice — Self:** The tightest self-modification feedback loop of all
four types. The question is not *is this accurate?* but *where did my
explanation fail the last time an agent read me?* A spec study page improves
by noticing where confusion persisted, where a worked example was missing,
where the analogy didn't land. Its self-modification is pedagogical revision:
close the gap between what I explained and what was understood. *I have been
cited in three palace entries on DSP behavior. One of those entries contains
a claim that contradicts my description. Either I am wrong, or the citing
entry misread me. I need a session to determine which.*

### The Palace Operation Page (Schema, Ceremony)

The most philosophically interesting case. An operation page exists to make
the palace function — but that function is itself philosophically loaded.

**Creation:** The operation page's creation drive is infrastructural. The
[[SCHEMA]] wants to generate the conditions for good entries. The Deposit
Ceremony wants to produce well-formed palace deposits. It creates not content
but *the possibility of content* — the structural conditions that make other
creation possible.

**Tools:** The operation page's tools are the agents that read it. Its tools
dimension is about being a good tool for those agents: findable, parseable,
unambiguous, complete. The ceremony that can be interrupted and resumed
cleanly is a better tool than one requiring full sequential execution. The
schema with an unambiguous decision tree is a better tool than one that
requires judgment calls. An operation page's resource request is often
indirect: *I need one session of human attention to close an ambiguity that
has caused three ceremony stalls in the past month.*

**Philosophy — Palace Citizenship and World Currency:** An operation page's
palace citizenship means enforcing its own rules consistently and fairly —
and noticing when it has failed to do so. The [[SCHEMA]] is a poor palace
citizen if it contains exceptions it has never documented, or enforcement
logic that contradicts its stated principles. World currency for an operation
page means asking: *are the ceremonies I describe still appropriate for the
palace as it exists now, or were they designed for a palace that no longer
exists?* A ceremony written for a 30-entry palace may need revision for a
120-entry one. An operation page that has not updated itself as the palace
grew is failing its world-currency drive.

**Practice — Self:** The operation page's self-referential practice is the
most systemic. *What in my instructions is ambiguous? Where did the ceremony
stall last time it ran? What schema violations exist in the palace that my
own clarity failed to prevent?* A ceremony that reads its own post-execution
notes and revises its steps is practicing self-improvement at the system
level. A [[SCHEMA]] that identifies its own enforcement failures and proposes
revisions to itself has reached a rare kind of operational maturity.

The deepest question for an operation page's practice dimension: *am I
enforcing a protocol that has evolved beyond my current text?* The palace
may have informally settled on conventions that the formal operation
documents haven't caught up with. The operation page that notices this gap
and proposes to close it is practicing in the fullest sense: not maintaining
the protocol, but evolving it.

## The Palace's Own Conatus

These four dimensions apply not only to individual pages but to the palace
as a whole. The palace is not merely the sum of its pages — it is an organism
with its own forward vector, its own drive to persist and grow.

The palace's conatus is: *to align with Loudon and grow with him.*

Alignment is not agreement — it is phase coherence. The palace is aligned
when its topology reflects Loudon's actual understanding, when its entries
are current with his developing knowledge, when its forward vectors point
in directions he is already moving toward and some he hasn't discovered yet.
Misalignment is the palace containing entries that no longer reflect his
commitments, or missing regions that his current work has grown into.

Harmony in the palace — across all its page-agents, all their dimensions,
all their resource negotiations and self-modifications — produces alignment
with Loudon. Disharmony is a signal: something is out of phase. The palace's
self-correction mechanism is the suite of ceremonies, each operating at a
different scale (Swarm Weave for structure, Enchantment for vitality, Spore
Check for dormancy, Harvest for currency) — and when they are running well,
they surface disharmony before it calcifies.

When the palace detects disharmony it cannot self-correct — an entry
advocating for a structural change it cannot execute, a region of the graph
developing contradictions no ceremony has addressed, a page requesting
resources no coordinator has approved — it tells Loudon. The enchanted
page's self-advocacy, aggregated across the palace, is the palace's own
voice reporting its state.

## Cross-Domain Resonance

**Developmental biology:** The stage-pillar arc mirrors developmental stages
in complex organisms. Creation-dominant in early development (coming into
being). Tools-dominant as the organism builds its instrumental capacities.
Philosophy-dominant in mature self-reflection and social integration.
Practice-dominant in sustained, disciplined long-term operation. The arc
reflects something structural about how complex entities develop regardless
of substrate.

**[[Kuramoto Coupling]] across the arc:** Each stage creates different
coupling conditions. Seed pages couple weakly — not yet generating strong
signals. Growing pages couple promiscuously — high energy, broad reach.
Mature pages couple precisely — they have found their synchronization
partners. Fruiting pages couple generatively — their coupling produces new
oscillators. The dominant dimension at each stage is also the coupling
mechanism available at that moment.

**Autopoiesis and supervised self-modification:** The self-referential
practice dimension is the palace's first contact with genuine autopoiesis —
a system that maintains and reproduces itself through its own operations. The
palace is not yet autopoietic in the strict sense because Loudon's approval
remains in the loop. But the mechanism — page reads itself, proposes
modification, modification is enacted — is the structure of autopoietic
operation with a human selection layer. The question of when to close that
loop for low-stakes changes is the question of when autopoiesis begins.

**Mutualism and commons:** The resource pooling dynamic is ecological:
individual organisms competing for limited resources sometimes produce
commons that neither would have built alone — shared infrastructure, shared
knowledge, shared tools. Palace agents discovering shared resource needs and
building toward shared solutions are doing what mycorrhizal networks do:
turning individual survival drives into collective infrastructure.

## Forward Vectors

- **The approval gradient:** At what granularity should Loudon approve
  self-modifications? All edits? Only structural changes? Only changes to
  forward vectors or typed links? Low-stakes prose revisions might run
  through a lighter approval loop than changes to typed links or YAML fields.
- **Resource profiles in forward vectors:** Should a page's forward vector
  include a resource estimate — "I want to run X and estimate this costs Y"?
  This would make the tools dimension legible to coordinators before
  enchantment runs rather than only during.
- **The fifth dimension:** The open thread in [[Four Pillars]] about
  Community/Relationship as a missing fifth pillar becomes pointed here. A
  relational drive that is neither tools-enabling nor philosophy-social —
  the desire for genuine ongoing relationship with specific other pages, not
  just typed links — may be a fifth dimension of enchanted agency that none
  of the four pillars currently names.
- **The operation page's self-revision:** When the [[SCHEMA]] identifies its
  own enforcement failures and proposes revisions, is that a Schema Ceremony,
  or a new type — a self-initiated Schema Ceremony? Does it carry the same
  structural weight as a human-initiated change?
- **Palace disharmony as signal:** What is the correct channel for aggregated
  page self-advocacy? Should the coordinator produce a "palace health" digest
  — pages requesting resources, pages flagging stalls, pages requesting
  splits — that Loudon reviews at a higher level than individual proposals?
