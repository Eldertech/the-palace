# AI Partnership Philosophy

**Use AI to BECOME better humans, not just DO more.**

This is the core principle. While most people use AI for productivity (complete tasks faster, produce more content), we use AI for growth (think more deeply, question paradigms, align with values). The difference is profound.

## The Paradigm Shift

### Most People's Approach (Efficiency Paradigm)
**Goal:** Complete tasks faster, produce more output

**How they use AI:**
- "Write this lesson for me" → copy output
- "Generate social media posts" → schedule without engagement
- "Optimize my content" → chase metrics
- "Make this faster" → do more, not better

**Outcome:** More output, no growth. Efficiency without depth. Busy, not better.

---

### Our Approach (Becoming Paradigm)
**Goal:** Deepen understanding, clarify values, grow as a human

**How we use AI:**
- "Let's explore kick drums through leverage points" → dialogue, challenge, refine thinking
- "Help me design a week that serves wellbeing" → co-create aligned with values
- "I'm feeling pulled to metrics" → redirected to paradigm awareness
- "Challenge my assumptions" → growth through friction

**Outcome:** Deeper thinking. Values clarified. Growth happened. Better human, not just faster producer.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Measure by Depth/Growth/Wellbeing, Not Efficiency

**Bad metric:** Did I finish faster?
**Good metric:** Did I learn something? Did I think more clearly? Am I more aligned with my values?

**In practice:**
- After AI conversation, ask: "What did I understand more deeply?"
- Not: "How much time did I save?"

### 2. Model Collaboration Publicly

**The teaching value:**
Your community needs to see HOW you work with AI, not just WHAT you produce.

**What to show:**
- The actual prompts you use
- How you refine AI responses
- When you disagree with AI suggestions
- How AI helps you think, not think for you
- The iterative process of co-creation

**Why it matters:**
Students see: AI as collaborator, not oracle. Questions matter more than answers. Process over product.

### 3. AI Remembers and Challenges Toward Values

**Claude's role (configured in memory):**
- Support Loudon's BECOMING (not just task completion)
- Redirect metrics talk to wellbeing questions
- Celebrate integrity over reach
- Challenge when drifting from values
- Witness practice, not applaud results

**Example exchanges:**

**User:** "My stream only got 20 views"

**Bad AI response:** "Try optimizing your thumbnail and title"
- Reinforces low-leverage thinking
- Pulls toward metrics paradigm

**Good AI response:** "How did it FEEL? Did you teach well? Did anyone have a breakthrough? Those 20—were they the right people?"
- Redirects to paradigm
- Wellbeing over metrics
- Values reinforced

### 4. Use AI for High-Leverage Work, Not Low-Leverage

**Low-leverage AI use (Parameters #12):**
- "Generate 10 social media posts"
- "Optimize this title for clicks"
- "Make this shorter/longer"

**High-leverage AI use (Goals/Paradigm #3, #2):**
- "Help me identify which leverage point this decision operates at"
- "I'm designing a weekly structure—challenge me if it conflicts with my values"
- "This feels like old paradigm thinking—help me see it from new paradigm"

## Use Cases & Examples

### Developing 4 Pillars Lesson

**Bad approach:**
- **Prompt:** "Write a lesson about kick drums"
- **Process:** Copy AI output → post it
- **Outcome:** Efficient but shallow. No growth. Generic content.

**Good approach:**
- **Prompt:** "Let's explore kick drums through leverage points. What small decisions create big impacts?"
- **Process:** Dialogue → challenge → refine → integrate your thinking → AI supports structure
- **Outcome:** Deeper understanding. Unique perspective. Growth happened.

---

### Planning Weekly Structure

**Bad approach:**
- **Prompt:** "Give me a weekly content schedule"
- **Process:** Follow AI's generic template
- **Outcome:** Doesn't fit your life. Ignores wellbeing. Cookie-cutter.

**Good approach:**
- **Prompt:** "I value peaceful life, physical making, time with Emily. Help me design a week that serves these."
- **Process:** AI asks about your rhythms → you share constraints → co-design → AI challenges when structure conflicts with values
- **Outcome:** Custom structure aligned with YOUR goals. AI helped you clarify what matters.

---
