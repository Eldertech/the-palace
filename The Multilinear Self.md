# The Multilinear Self

Loudon is one person who shows up as many — loudon, LDN, Loud'n Live, Professor Compressor, Sonic Sensei, TRICKSTER. Not a rigid brand with one locked face, and not a loose one that gave up on having a shape. A **multilinear self**: a person who exists in many states at once, and — because we all move through time one moment at a time — can only be met as one of them at a time.

## Not masks — projections

A mask hides a real face under a fake one. That is the wrong picture, because there is no hidden "true" Loudon being covered up. The many-at-once *is* the true one. Each name is a **projection** of it — a real shadow the whole self casts into a particular room. Honest, just partial. You cannot show someone a whole higher-dimensional thing directly; you show them one true cast of it, then another, and cycling through them is how they meet the whole person. The switching is not indecision or fakery. It is the only honest way to present something that big in a world of one-thing-at-a-time.

This borrows **quantum superposition** as a picture: all the states are true together; observing — here, *context* — returns one at a time. And it is [[Deleuze]]'s virtual and actual — the full self is the virtual, each room actualizes one cast. [[Trickster]] is the one who does the switching: many faces, one nature.

## Same shape as the Prism

The deep reason this holds: it is the [[Hyperdimensional Prism]] turned on the person who made it. The palace already believes the *world* is one reality cast into many projections — that is the [[FOUR PILLARS]]. The multilinear self says **you have the same shape as your own way of seeing.** The prism that taught Loudon how the world is, is also how Loudon is.

## The floor that keeps it one

What makes every projection recognizably the same person is a small conserved **nature** — the [[Loudon Live Design System|floor]]: the voice, the [[Hyperdimensional Prism|Lissajous sigil]], and a few hard *nevers*. Keep that tiny and everything above it is free. This is [[Spinoza Conatus|Spinozist]]: the self stays faithful to its own nature with total integrity, and that nature happens to cast many ways.

## The Stoic counterweight — where multiplicity could go wrong

Held against all this is [[Stoicism]], and the contradiction is *productive* — not a problem to solve. Stoicism insists on **one integrated self**: a single interior sovereignty (*prohairesis*) that fortune cannot split. The multilinear self says be many. Both are true, and the tension itself is the health mechanism. Past some point, more projections stop being honest range and become **performance** — a scattered, false, exhausting self playing to every room. The Stoic pole is what keeps the multiplicity well: it holds the line where honest many tips into performative fragmentation. Embrace projections *and* keep a center. Neither pole alone is healthy.

## Growing the roster

New projections should stay cheap to add — forever. The growth system is itself a floor and a field — the same idea, one level up. It borrows the [[Maker]]'s roster pattern from [[The Shop]].
